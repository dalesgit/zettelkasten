Each resource is anchored by an essential question and features video clips from the film, teaching tips, and discussion questions for deeper classroom engagement. What was it like to live in 18th-century North America? Explore the daily lives, economies, and demographics that defined the 13 British colonies before the war for independence. These resources also help students learn more about Native nations before the American Revolution, as well as the complex relationships that existed between Native peoples and colonists.

Help your students understand the political, economic, and societal factors that led the colonies to declare their independence from Great Britain. These classroom resources will encourage them to explore the precipitating events that played significant roles in igniting a revolution.
