Things to Do in Charleston

**Rainbow Row** is a must-see, featuring 13 pastel-colored historic homes along East Bay Street, known for their vibrant architecture and rich history. Just a short walk away, **The Battery** offers scenic waterfront views, cannons from the Civil War, and grand Southern mansions.

Explore **Fort Sumter**, accessible by ferry from downtown, where the Civil War began. For deeper history, visit **The International African American Museum (IAAM)** at Gadsden’s Wharf, a powerful new addition that explores African American heritage.

Wander through **Charleston City Market**, a bustling hub of local crafts and food, or stroll through **Waterfront Park** and admire the iconic **Pineapple Fountain**. For nature lovers, the **Angel Oak Tree**, a 400–500-year-old giant just 30 minutes outside the city, is an unforgettable sight.

Take a **plantation tour** at **Boone Hall**, **Magnolia Plantation**, or **Middleton Place**, each offering historical insight, gardens, and wildlife. **Drayton Hall**, one of America’s oldest unrestored plantations, is also highly recommended.

For unique experiences, try a **ghost tour**, **harbor cruise**, or **kayaking** in the Folly River. Don’t miss **Sullivan’s Island Beach** or **Folly Beach** for coastal relaxation.

**Free things to do**: Strolling downtown, visiting **Rainbow Row**, **Waterfront Park**, **Angel Oak**, and **Charleston City Market**.
