**Information Age**  

Information we got in bucket loads,\
data out the gazooloo.\
It's the age we live in,\
So they say. So\
the questions we ask in this age\
seek information, so\
"Wiki" is the magical tool here ,\
like a toy guillotine.

Information is power and control.\
Information is money.
Today.

Yesterday I sought wisdom, while\
Information was a by-product.

Ancient Greece understood a hierarchy of knowledge:\
(_from least to best_)

- opinion 
- true belief
- justified true belief [This is information from](https://www.britannica.com/topic/epistemology/The-history-of-epistemology)
- scientific knowledge [we've thought about this](https://the-past.com/feature/ancient-greeks-science-and-wisdom/)
- mathematics
- wisdom

To know [what we do not know](https://iep.utm.edu/ancient-greek-philosophy/)\
Is a lost art\
Can you imagine?\
Should our leaders have **wisdom**?\
Such a quaint thought.\
Information we got in bucket loads,\
Power and control the name of the game.\
What a world we live in.
