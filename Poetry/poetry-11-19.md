Hilton Head with Amy

Out our third floor window are tree branches
Not a car to be seen.
I've scarcely dwelt on the national chaos 
Spending time trying to solve the puzzle
of automating daily, weekly, monthly pages
in Obsidian. 
Slow **agonizing** progress.
Black. Black is the theme. 
light, light, the dream.
She rode my bike. She hugged me "night, night."
She put a warm blanket around my heart
without trying at all.
