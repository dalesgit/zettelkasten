The smell of bread baking melts my defenses.
"Happy Clappy" he called it, expecting a fellow conspirator.
I'm not.
If there's an old-time warrior with whom I identify 
it's Dan Berrigan.
Also the speaker who said that,
"black churches" were more like each other
than the denominations to which they belonged.

I tried to learn guitar for years,
from simple folk, to classical, to bluesy wannabe.
The best I ever got was callouses on my fingers.
"Expect that what I'm saying is ironic," I said, 
"until demonstrated otherwise."
The lyrics I loved were just that:
Randy Newman, 
the singer on tuba,
… ?

I was a sucker for the Doors and Janis Joplin.
They were suckers for pharmaceuticals and
taught me of the dead-end of that path,
as did that spirit-sister of Beni-Hanna lineage.
Too soon gone. Chapin with his deep wisdom and generosity.
Lennon with his combination of all of the above.

I'm not looking to advance the human race
like I did at 20.
Not really looking to advance myself
at an age decline is all around me.
What I seek is to fill my 24 / 7,
my 1440, with as much integrity as I can rustle up.

I see the rising Louis XIV epoch.
Integrity has lost its meaning. 
The "nice" behavior of most Christians
distorts to dissolution the iconography of Jesus on the Cross.
No, "nice" I don't have time for.

What are the memories that give my life meaning?

- the AIDS lad who left me his autobiography
- homeless Susan
- the homeless couple in the Bible study group
- the Easter Vigil that by testimony was over too-quickly
- the woman dying from sepsis, who stopped thrashing 
when the healing of prayer came over her.
- the smile on the "Queen of the Children's section" at her last breath

My father said a **gentleman** can eat as comfortably with the highest
as with the lowest.
Today the highest are far too full of themselves to allow me comfort.
My heart is with the unsung, the overlooked, the poorly dressed,
or not at all, 
the hungry and the forsaken.

I could be **happy clappy** for them.
