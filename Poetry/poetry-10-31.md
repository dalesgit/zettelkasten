I dream I am an artist\
A purposeful swath with the brush\
followed by a commanding line \
penned to find a shape\
that reveals and creates.\
It is a longing or an envy\
a dream

I wake to grapple with my enemy envy.\
Jealous of a Picasso's ability with a line drawing?\
Unfulfilled desire to be like a satisfied ex-President?\
Envy bad! Acceptance good!\
Such is the battle.

What is the desire?\
Self-fulfillment? \
To share the newly minted?

I think it's more like a reminder\
To myself\
That I am alive.\
Carl Reiner reported that he reads the obituaries\
To see if he has died.\
I am alive.
