Shane Claiborn cries "No Christian could …"
It's no good crying "Faith, faith" where the meaning is:
Comfort, comfort my people.
Remember equals nostalgia.
Does Christian faith come down to:
I believe in an afterlife,
While Jews "obviously don't?"
The golden here-after is a mirage.
"All shall be well" I get,
Even with the Black Plague all around me.
Resurrection means how I live, How I trust, How I give myself,
In the midst of L'Étranger.
