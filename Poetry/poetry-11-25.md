**To capture my attention**

Is it like a mouse trap?
Or more like a fly trap?
Is it trapping at all or
more like setting free the encumbered?
  
Why is it the periwinkle
that stubbornly insists on crossing the sidewalk can
one day call my attention while
the next I mindlessly step over it?
  
To capture my attention
is more than just
leading me to notice.
Somehow I am in chains.
  
Is it my attention
needs capturing or
is it the I who
asks who needs?
  
When you've captured my attention
do I know your name?
Do you call my name to get my attention
like a daughter who cries out the name of her Daddy?
  
Is it the need that captures?
Or the intonation of the voice?
Is it you who cries for me?
Or I you?
  
You have my attention.
Now what?
An embrace?
Tears?
  
A pause in Time?
  
I and Thou
cry out together.
I disappear and
only You remain.
