I code 
Like saying I fly
Because I rode an airplane once.
Meaning
I write in ASCII.
If you don't know, that's "plain text";
then I convert to the format I want.

I regret to this day the time capsule
I packed 23 years ago 
Full of obsolete objects 
That will be unknown to the recipients.

I write
Like saying I'm a writer.
I remember the panic I felt,
Newly ordained, and thought,
"The rest of my life I will succeed or fail
based on the words I speak."

I write
Because I feel more alive 
when I try to find meaning 
By slipping these words into some particular order
In order to uncover this meaning.
