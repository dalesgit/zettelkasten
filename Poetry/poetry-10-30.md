I code 
Like saying I fly
Because I rode an airplane once.
But there's something there
I write in ASCII
If you don't know, that's "plain text";
Then I convert to the format I want.

I regret to this day the time capsule
I packed 23 years ago 
Full of obsolete objects 
That will be unknown to the recipients.
