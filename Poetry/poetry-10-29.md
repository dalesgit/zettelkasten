When the morning is filled with distraction\
I forget who I am\
Our world screams at us for attention\
Moment by moment\
We relinquish our humanity.\
In an earlier stage of my life\
I provided the intensity\
Trying to demonstrate my mettle in the world.\
I took the touch of an honest companion for granted.\
Now that I am old \

Michelangelo's touch has been bred out of us:
- smartphones
- connectivity
- algorithms for power and control
- opinion replaces gnosis

Who am I? Where am **I?**
