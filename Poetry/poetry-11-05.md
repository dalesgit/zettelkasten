I saw a gun once
What a curious accessory 
It's not useful to making friends.
Hardly effective at building community.
My life-long quest to find a
companion on the way to the sacred
finds no satisfaction or reassurance
in this thing that is built for destruction.

How to be friends with a wrecking ball?
I guess if they payed you money 
in exchange for lubrication of health or community.

What's a gun for?
For power and control.
For intimidation.

You say for hunting
But it's really **sport** 
Isn't it, 
in a world of supermarkets.
