In my waking dream I was working hard
to make things right
It was a teaching environment
Working with others was the challenge
Yet. yet, yet yet.
I didn't want to leave that place

The fragments that we weave together
Take flight and live
Whither thou goest none to know
Or sink and swelter in the heat of brew
Stoking and simmering 
mostly speechless
mostly unsettling
mostly unnerving

So weave this day a tale of wonder
Alive
