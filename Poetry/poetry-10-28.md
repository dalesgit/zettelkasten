**Storms a-brewing**

We live in Florida afterall
It's the story of the place
Storms, named and un-named,
The worst of the year is off to the east 
Squalls jumping from place to place
Rain comes and goes 
Then finally the wind becomes fierce
While things begin to break.

It's all a metaphor for the
Signs of the times.
Words lose their meaning 
When yes means no
And up means down.
We've heard it before, 
over and over again.
Are you paying attention?
Are you ready for the house to come tumbling down?
