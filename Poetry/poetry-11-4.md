Precious moments of the early morning\
I breathe the night air\
As hints of light begin to appear\
The night sounds of silence\
slowly give way to the marching cadence of the day

O blessed night that nourishes\
From dark recesses of the universe\
**Stardust** \
that fuels and lights the creation\
and emanates from a\
brass candlestick in hand

I hold creation itself in hand\
a brief unmeasurable moment\
of awareness\
ירשא\
blessed happiness.
