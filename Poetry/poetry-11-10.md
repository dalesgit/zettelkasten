8 Democrats broke ranks today.
My stomach sinks at the news
to my surprise
since my conviction that 
all political solutions
are over-priced and over-hyped
but most certainly in light of eternity
and even with much shorter frame.

Our tyrant in residence
commands the stage and
since all the world's a stage
he's bigger than life.

Yesterday the pleasantly decorous church group
maintained the need to build bridges,
bemoaned the woeful scene of broken relationships,
took for granted the obligation of all good followers of Jesus
to be known as the "reconcilers" on stage.

O you spineless 
where's your conviction?
Isn't life wonderful!
"All are accepted, no exceptions."
Perhaps especially the complicit who
seek most of all their own well-being.

Me and my fellow Handmaidens are angry.
