St. Nicholas: Shoes at the door: MP remembers and I think Bridget did too. Foreign to me.

Poem: **Memorare** into _anamnesis_

I met him long ago when we were still young
It was in the context of memorizing poetry

"You are here to kneel
where prayer hs been valid"

So begins a week of commemorations:

Memories
Observances

Ferrar, Pearl harbor, Mary (Immaculate Conception), Ordination, Anniversary

In all my years to date I've learned one thing pretty clearly. **Chronos** is a very poor measure of **Kairos**.
