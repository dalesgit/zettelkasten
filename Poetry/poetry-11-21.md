I put together a dozen or some pieces
to [[MP]]'s puzzle-gift.
I'm so excited by the energy 
_The Grammar of Fantasy_ generates within me.
Rodari cut his teeth on children's stories
but I feel the deep connection with Jesus's
knowing that learning comes through stories.
Wiesel reminded us that God loves humans
because God loves stories.
How can anyone miss that the cosmos
is made up of stories?

> Science's concept of the universe was changed irrevocably by quantum mechanics and Einstein's theory of relativity which questioned the separate identity of energy and matter. Our comfortable ideas of a universe made up of solid little bits of matter behaving in logical ways have been exploded. A particle is not a separate entity but a set of relationships. The world is an interconnected tissue of events, a dynamic unbroken whole.
> Gary Zukov, *The Dancing Wu-Li Masters*

"A tissue of events" is the very definition of a story.
