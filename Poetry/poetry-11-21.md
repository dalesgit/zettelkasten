I put together a dozen or pieces
to [[MP]]'s puzzle-gift.
I'm so excited by the energy 
_The Grammar of Fantasy_ generates
Rodari cut his teeth on children's stories
but I feel the deep connection with Jesus's
knowing that learning comes through stories
Wiesel reminded us that God loves humans
because God loves stories.
