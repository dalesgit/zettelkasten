The ease of the familiar hides the encounter
which disarms and reveals
while alluring and alarming
the charm of the ordinary.
While seeking the surprise factor
of Grace itself
I risk an adrenaline fix
that closes while 
exposing the insincerity of the surface
in which we too commonly live.
_Ordinary lives_, ordinary people,
that's where Grace operates
and parables thrive.
The route to plain living 
is often counterfeit graffiti
not simple grace.
Grace in the Ordinary I seek above all.
