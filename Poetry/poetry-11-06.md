As a student I read
Whitehead I think
the notion we are only aware of our bodies
when something is working.
I suspect there may be some
English _stiff upper lip_ stuff going on there.

After accepting that for 50 years
I'm quite certain that self-awareness is much more than that
though it may be a lot of working to
know what we don't know.

So, too, the body - soul divide.
The transcendent God.
So much that seemed like a 
"Prudential Life" like granite peak 
Now seems like a mistake-filled path
that leads to nowhere in particular.

Where did you go Charlie Brown
and other certainties that sustained
our years of toil and purpose-filled searching?

What a wonderful world
