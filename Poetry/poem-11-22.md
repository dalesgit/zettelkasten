So much there is I do not know.
Such a big space for "ah, hah!"
God speaks in Serendipity
which is to say via "Surprise"
To live a life of expectation of surprise.
How related, I might ask, to a life of hope?
I feel pulled beneath the surface of the water 
By the voices that say, "You mustn't give up hope."
There's the "You should'a ..." part
but beyond that the head-in-the-sand,
Pollyanna saccharine polish
that prompts me to say,
"Show me your wound."
[[Linda]] said, "Creativity can be part of the resistance."
I wonder if, "I didn't know that ..." might be part too?
