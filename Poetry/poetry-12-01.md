Whence come these captains
Wanting rule of my ways?
Truth, Honesty, Integrity
seek my acquiescence
though unbidden
and for gain's sake
not worth their weight
as meagre as it is?

I didn't bid them
take the measure of my living,
how much less my dying.
Do I owe it to my parents?
One I knew by his absence;
the other seemed fickle and whimsical by turns.

Today I see these officers
chiefly in the witness of the rulers of the today
who know them not,
Slaves to the intoxicant of power.
Doest thou seek to rule through weakness?

A scrawny tenpin
to the rumble of the mighty ball
sent rolling down the arteries
of the economies by which
we eat, drink, and shelter?

Truth, Honesty, Integrity.
A trinity of umpires shaping the game of life 
we didn't choose
but consume with either gratitude
or violent sneer.

Are we ravished maidens or
Raped Madonnas?
The villain or the valiant?
Power or weakness is the choice
I see and hear.
But there is another who whispers more than shouts.
It is a finger touching finger
that promises more than orders.
It is the Pietá where violence and tenderness join
in embrace.
It is a Cheyenne brave knelt 
down upon a berm
to scout the inexhaustible prairie
And I stood there with him in solidarity
several centuries later.

Power is like grass. 
A dime a dozen.
Thoughtlessly swept aside.
Waving without principle or order
with the wind or storm, 
rain or sleet.
Here today and gone tomorrow.

Still the captains dwell.
