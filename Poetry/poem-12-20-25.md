Listening for the sound of your voice
It takes some quiet. Early morning will do.
Often a story vaults to the head of the line,
Especially when it grows out of love.
What a throw-away word. 
It carries too much freight or none at all.
I read this morning about a network of truckers,
Thousands who daily look out for stranded motorists.
Lives saved, connections made, numbering in the thousands.
Friends. Across the divides we build.
