I am weary in my bones from a tiny project
To begin the effort it takes
to put the house into some kind of order
after months of upheaval from the bathroom renovation.

In the background [[MP]] heads a Zoom session of
_Centering Prayer_® followup workshop.

Have I fed the birds today?
In the end my mother found meaning in her life 
in the winged preparation and execution 
of feeding a flock birds that came to her 
morning and evening.

My spiritual director regularly listens to me ask, 
"What's it all about?" (_Alfie_, from the movie)
She regularly helps me see the answer is usually pretty simple.
"Creation can be Resistance" (to the tyranny)

To find a little harmony in the things about me.
To set in order in such a way that I find a little peace.
A little script to ease the little tasks.
I remember the marvel I felt when my boss showed us a prototype of a digital clock to be used by airplane pilots.
The empowerment in putting my checkbook into a spreadsheet.
So quaint, so shortly ago.
And today we face Armageddon at the hands of A-rtificial I-ntelligence.

Today the birds have a piece of satisfaction.
Thanks be to God.
