I didn't know her well.\
We were band mates.\
She played flute\
(or perhaps it was clarinet)\
She was not a Beatrice,\
but I know now she was an angel.

We were just 17.\
The last time I could remember her name\
Was decades ago.\
I've never forgotten her though.\
She gave me a priceless gift.

This particular day
She passed to me a folded page,
like a school child passing a "secret" note.
I unfolded to read what seemed 
like a strange hieroglyphics.

"We are the hollow men 
We are the stuffed men 
Leaning together
Headpiece filled with straw. !**Alas**
… "

Through the years I've wanted to tell her "thank you".
I've wanted to know her name.
Eliot's words sparked 5a fire within me.
Such that now at age 75
I still spontaneously emote
"Let us go then, you and I … "
