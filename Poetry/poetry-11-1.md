I didn't know her well.
We were band mates.
She played flute
(or perhaps it was clarinet)
She was not a Beatrice,
but I know now that she was an angel.
We were just 17.
The last time I could remember her name
Was decades ago.
I've never forgotten her though.
She gave me a priceless gift.
