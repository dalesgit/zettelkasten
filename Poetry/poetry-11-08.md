# poetry-11-8 

How do I rest? Always on the hunt?
For what? Why am I so restless?
Feel irritated by all the "oughta's" I follow
day by day?
Night time is not so restful,
A time to silently rehearse whatever is stubbornly stuck in the frontal lobe?
