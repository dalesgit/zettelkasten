It's the economy stupid.
A lesson from 30 years ago.
I think for me it's
It's the narrative stupid.

Watching TV -- Lydia said,
"You never used to do that." -- 
So she remembers that! --
I am very interested in what I describe as
"How they tell the story."

Others are interested in the movie stars.
I'm interested in the story.
I remember the period in the 70's when I was so interested in the genre
**Melodrama**.

Margaret Atwood's TV series
Stretches beyond the book  -- the sequel?
