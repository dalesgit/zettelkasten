**Wachet auf mein Herr**.
Wake up, the time is near.
Is it for Anticipation? Emancipation?
Duty or Performance? 
How's your insides, man?

It's your responsibility, sir.
You'll let them down if you don't, sir.
Perhaps it'll be your **dénouement**, sir.
"This is the end, my friend."
The one we've all been waiting for.

This is not trivial, mein Herr.
It's not like, "this too will pass."
This counts. Doubt me not.
It's time for **awareness**. Eyes open.
Pay attention. This is important

If it's true.
