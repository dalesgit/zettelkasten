## Trains

- failure to invest in public transportation
- Sandburg "Chicago"
- the prowess and hubris of engineers
- perfect form of transportation
- except in US

I have by executive proclamation 
often pronounced that
Trains are the best form of travel, though
Not in the USA, of course.

Trains came to London in the mid 1800's,
a feat of engineering, transforming the 
ecology and the ethos of the environment.
Such an accomplishment, fellas --
it was only fellas in those days.

The US had trains, once,
But the Goodyear® and Standard Oil® guys
made an end to that in the mid 20th c.
Rubber and highways the new venue for engineers' strutting.

I read a book once, called _Blue Highways_.
It sang of the **grace in community** to be found
along the forgotten by-ways of the land.
I tried to follow the trail one summer.

I had a friend who used to say of another bright acquaintance,
"He's forgotten more than I know."
Our land has forgotten more than it knows now.
Where did it go, the **grace in community** we once knew?
"The City of New Orleans" crooned Arlo, 
and it still breaks my heart when I hear it.
The way of the trains.

We failed to invest in "public transportation."
Who is the **public** but **we** the public?
Who took the "Last Train to Clarksville?"
And it's never comin' back.
