## Shortbread recipe

- prep time: 15-20
- cook time: 15-30
- servings: 16-44
- temperature: 325-350

## Ingredients

- unsalted butter: 1 cup
- powdered sugar: ½ cup, … ¾ cup, ⅓ cup
- granulated sugar: ⅔ cup, … ½ cup, ¾ cup
- all-purpose flour: 4c; … 2c; 2½c; 
- cornstarch: 2T …
- salt: ¼t
- vanilla bean seeds
- bittersweet chocolate (for drizzle)


Shortbread cookies are a classic, buttery, and tender treat traditionally originating in Scotland, known for their crumbly texture and simple ingredient list. They are made without leavening agents, relying on high fat content for their rich, melt-in-your-mouth quality.

- Prep Time: 15–20 minutes
    
- Cook Time: 10–35 minutes (varies by recipe)
    
- Servings: 12–48 (varies by recipe)
    
- Temperature: 300°F (150°C) to 350°F (180°C) (varies by recipe)
    

**Ingredients:**

- **Butter**: 1 cup (227g) to 2 cups (454g), softened or at room temperature 
    
- **Powdered (Icing) Sugar**: 3/4 cup (90g) to 1 cup (113g) 
    
- **Granulated Sugar**: 1 cup (200g) 
    
- **Vanilla Extract**: 1–2 teaspoons (5–10ml) 
    
- **All-Purpose Flour**: 2 to 4 cups (240–500g), depending on recipe 
    
- **Corn Starch**: 1/2 cup (60g), optional (used in some recipes for tenderness) 
    
- **Fine Grain Sea Salt**: 1 1/4 teaspoons (7g), optional (used in some recipes) 
    
- **Optional Add-ins**: Maraschino cherries, gumdrops, almond extract, lemon zest, or herbs 
    

**Equipment Needed:**

- Stand mixer or hand mixer
    
- Measuring cups and spoons
    
- Mixing bowls
    
- Parchment paper or silicone baking mats
    
- Baking sheets
    
- Rolling pin (if not using a cookie press)
    
- Cookie cutters (if shaping)
    
- Fork (for docking dough)
    
- Wire cooling rack
    

**Instructions:**

1. Preheat the oven to the temperature specified in the recipe (300°F to 350°F).
    
2. Prepare baking sheets by lining them with parchment paper or silicone mats.
    
3. In a large bowl, cream the softened butter and sugars together until light and fluffy, about 3–4 minutes. For recipes using cold butter, cream until soft before adding other ingredients.
    
4. Stir in vanilla extract and any additional flavorings (e.g., almond extract).
    
5. Gradually add the flour (and corn starch, if using), mixing until a smooth or crumbly dough forms. Some recipes require the dough to hold its shape when squeezed.
    
6. If using a cookie press, fill it with dough and form cookies onto the prepared sheets, spacing them 1–1.5 inches apart. If rolling, roll the dough to 1/3–3/4 inch thick and cut into shapes using a cookie cutter. For flat rounds, press the dough into greased or parchment-lined cake pans.
    
7. For larger cookies, use a fork to prick the surface (docking) to prevent bubbling during baking.
    
8. Bake until the edges are just starting to turn golden brown (10–15 minutes for pressed cookies) or until deeply golden where they touch the pan (20–35 minutes for rolled or pan-baked cookies).
    
9. Remove from the oven and cool on the baking sheet for 5–10 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
