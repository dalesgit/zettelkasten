## First day of travel {#First day of travel}

MP read to me about an upcoming documentary by Carl Reiner, about
nonogenarians like himself. He is currently writing 2 books and he has
written at least 1 a year for some time. He advised people about living
to an old age to have a goal when they wake up in the morning. Title of
his piece, or quote, "He gets up in the morning and reads the
obituaries. If he's not listed then he eats breakfast."
