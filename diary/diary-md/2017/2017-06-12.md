## Arriving Sedona {#Arriving Sedona}

We made a trip mostly on blue highways, leaving the interstate after
about 50 miles. We traveled through Apache country toward Globe -- the
launching place for the highway to Ray, Kearny & Hayden. Beyond that we
went through the "Tonto National Forest", Roosevelt Reservoir, and
lots of siguaros. It was a beautiful ride, above the heat and oppressive
atmosphere of the valley from Tucson to Phoenix.

Mary Pat is getting better.
