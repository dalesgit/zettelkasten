# Visit yesterday with Joe and Joan {#Visit yesterday with Joe and Joan}

In the midst of talk of education, need for change, etc. They made
reference to how Joan had perceived that the Soviet system had produced
a population that could not do problem solving. That, of course, didn't
fit with the examples we were giving that found that our system was
producing such a result.

At one point Joe said that since he was a business person, he was in
favor of charter schools because competition was the way to achieve
favorable results.

One thing and another and I woke up in the middle of the night with the
thought that the failure of *laisseze faire* capitalism was that it can
not produce []{#justice}**justice**.

I wonder if that is true? Could it be tested?
