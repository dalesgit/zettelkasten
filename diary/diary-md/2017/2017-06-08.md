It is now the 3rd day of coughing for Mary Pat. It hasn't got better
-- maybe worse. And I had to conclude this morning that we will need to
get a motel room in Las Cruces to protect Mom. That will lead to various
interesting consequences.

Yesterday we traveled from the []{#South}**South** to the
[]{#West}**West**, Louisiana to Abilene. We were just past Vicksburg in
Louisiana, the Mississippi delta environment all around. I didn't
really know what was a "bayou" but I think we were in the midst of it.

the Johnson ranch, I learned about a line that runs north and south
somewhere to the west of Dallas/Ft. Worth where precipitation drops off
and one enters a desert-like environment.
