# Day 1 Atlanta {#Day 1 Atlanta}

It is Pentecost. The receptionist yesterday asked us if we were going to
church today! I told her that was a first for me. But when I explained
about Pentecost and baptism of my children she clearly didn't have a
clue.

> Dear children, Everyday I pray for my children, each of you in your
own ways are courageously forging the life you are called to live.
Generally I add, "The ones who talk to me and the ones who don't." On
this, the anniversary of baptism for all of you, I can simply rejoice to
*remember* each of you on the occasion of your baptism. Right now we're
in Atlanta and the receptionist, who attends a famous
[]{#mega-church}**mega-church** in the neighborhood, and, while being a
passionate Christian in her own style, she didn't have a clue what I
was talking about when I shared a little of your story. Today I rejoice
and celebrate the life of each one of you. Thanks be to God.
[]{#P.S.}**P.S.**: I would be grateful for those of you who know Emma's
contact information that you would pass on this greeting and blessing.
Thank you.
