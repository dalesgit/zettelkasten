# Give us this day {#Give us this day}

Mon Jul 27, 2020 02:54 PM

In today\'s morning prayer we read from a little bit of the prophet
Hosea. It provided an image of God feeding his children. The first image
that came to my mind was of the children I literally fed from the time
of their infancy. You are one of those children. I am keenly aware of
how at a certain point I stopped feeding you \-- literally. I thought
that it also could be construed figuratively. I stopped feeding you
figuratively at some other time. I wondered, \"How might I feed you
now?\" Honestly I have no ideas. But I\'m sending you this little
thought, and imagining myself saying, \"Hey! Hi!\" Not much, I know. But
I do love you. Dad.
