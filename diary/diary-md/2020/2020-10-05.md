# First day of counseling

cf. notes in *Joplin*

The day after. Awake early to go to my first counseling at 8am.
Conversation of one kind or another with a pretty good range of people
yesterday, the longest were phone calls with Bill and with Scott. Irony
and tension. The most satisfying? Perhaps texting with Lydia and with
Mo. Neither of them particularly deep but each of them felt like the
other wanted to hear from me.

By far the most important element of the day was the incredible amount
of effort that :Mary Pat: had put into the meal, the card, the overall
impact of the day. It was a beautiful effort. Love.

# Journaling

The counselor sent me material about journaling. I haven't looked at it
yet. But, late, after Mary Pat has gone and fetched Sean and I have
retreated into the bedroom, I begin reading Sue Monk Kidd. It is her
journal from when she was maybe in her 30's. The beginning of her
awakening. At the beginning she announces she wants to become a writer.
She tries to divide her life into spheres and apportion out her
responsibilities in each. I thought, "What she is going through is
analogous to what I am going through with Sean." He is like a child
added to the family. My challenge is for finding my own space.

The t-shirt Mary Pat gave me says, in effect, that I have to find time
for playing piano. Writing, too.
