# Scott Jansen called {#Scott Jansen called}

Scott called. We had a 1 1/2 hour call. He is on a sleep apnea machine
every day. He has to augment with oxygen because of his lung damage.

Main reason for the call was to report that he got a call from Brian
that \"he is an alcoholic and needs help.\" But he is afraid of losing
his job if he seeks help. Will he lose his wife and child? and One on
the way.

Searching for a response, I connected the theme of powerlessness to the
sometimes impossible task of being a parent. Thinking of Emma in
particular, I remember the way in which they were my sun and moon. My
love ran and continues to run very deep. And yet I\'m powerless to
overcome the distance between us. Just so Scott is powerless to do
anything to []{#save}**save** Brian.

There will be things along the way he can do, however, like accompany
him to an AA meeting. Look up and confirm that there is an EAP for his
company. He can provide \"brutal honesty.\"
