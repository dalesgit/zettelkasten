# National Cathedral Good Friday {#National Cathedral Good Friday}

The reader of Isaiah totally reminded me of David Jocelyn. He was the
only one dressed just in a cassock. He was obviously a trusted reader
for the cathedral. His intonation and phrasing, his earnestness, and his
evident kindness all seemed like David.

He took the part of Jesus in the Passion, just as David so often did.

The Dean\'s voice and cadence, even appearance, reminds me of Frank
Gray. At a time that Frank seemed less poisonous to me.
