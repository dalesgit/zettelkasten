# Sue & Epi's wedding today

I haven't done well with journaling or with food journaling. I feel like
life has run away and I'm floundering like a diseased marine mammal.

Yesterday morning as we went on a fruitless quest for Sean's car I still
felt like I had been manipulated and bitter, with overtones of Emma's
response to me. Emma makes me feel, "Fuck it."

The hour drive in the back seat was actually pretty peaceful and I got
to look at the scenery -- something I really have an opportunity to do.
The houses revealed such a range of social standing: abandoned shacks,
rusted out trailers for sale, old established farm buildings, million
dollar spreads tucked away behind a bank of pines, upscale neighborhoods
erected on what would have been agricultural lands.

The internet has come and gone over the last 12 hours. Where are my
prompts?
