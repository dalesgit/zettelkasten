    # This is us

    Beth's father said to her, "Our lives are built on the tragedies
    that define our lives." What do you do with the terrible things?
    Make lemonade? As it was being spoken I made of it that it is the
    pillars of the major sadnesses of our lives that our decisions are
    fashioned into the future. Always we are defending the Maginot line.
    Two different takes.

    Randall is dealing with "Black lives matter". His siblings are too
    oblivious to really reckon with the reality. They are dealing with
    major events in their lives, as they seek some reconciling act: Kate
    getting a new baby from adoption, Kevin finding a spouse and a
    pregnancy with a boy and a girl.

As we watched it seemed unfortunate and somehow "telling" that Sean
didn't want anything to do with watching *This is us*. All the values
important to us that he rejects. The focus is on himself.
