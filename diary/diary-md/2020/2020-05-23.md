# Open source to Riley {#Open source to Riley}

Open-source is probably the biggest factor. By that I don\'t mean
\"free\". Goes back to the line that there\'s free as in beer \-- and
free as in speech. Open-source software is free speech. A good case can
be made that the world runs on open-source software \-- even though it
is closed-source outfits that have monopolized and made money. But it
ends up flexible. Lots of options. In some cases there is more
opportunity than in closed-source. Other times one has to settle for \"a
little more awkward.\" But I would say that everything you can do with
closed-source you can do better in open-source. But you may have to work
a little harder. An example is that I am currently taking a
certification course on Blackboard so that Winthrop University will let
me teach online \-- even though I have been doing it since 2011. The
fact is that because of the work I have done in open-source software I
can make Blackboard do things that most people can\'t imagine. Another
goes back 15 years or so. I used to use a program called Leo. It was a
versatile program for managing information. There was a glitch that I
couldn\'t solve in an export I was trying to accomplish. I emailed the
programmer \-- who lived in France. He responded within hours and by the
end of the day had patched the program so that it worked. It was so cool
being connected like that. · Reply
