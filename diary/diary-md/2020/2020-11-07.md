not surprisingly, fared much worse.

me to provide for you and to engage myself in something worthwhile.

Chaminade. I even knew of and talked to several Episcopal priests who

provide for a college education for both you and Lydia. The contract
included a provision for my children to get a full-ride to Chaminade
University. Sadly, by that time, you and Lydia had decided that you
