# Precipitates and elephants

Thu Oct 22, 2020 05:27 AM

Yesterday I had a whole new level of understanding the gulf between us.
I really didn't appreciate it adequately. Mary Pat was retaliating, I
think, against the time a day or two previous when she was casually
referring to some way in which our calendar that day was determined by
Sean's schedule, and I cut off conversation by saying, "That's a Sean
conversation that we can't pursue right now."

So we were in the bathroom. I was grooming somehow. She was getting out
of the WC. She made some kind of comment about not liking her hair. I
made what was intended as a light-hearted comment, thinking of the many
women, mostly women of color, who have shaved their heads -- often but
not always in support of breast cancer solidarity -- I said, "you could
shave your head."

She responded by gathering a very stern face and walking away, saying
something to the effect, "The word 'shave' was the occasion for an major
argument we had; so we can't have that conversation."

What she was recalling was a time that also caught me by surprise. Again
she was talking about her hair and she made a reference to "shaving her
head", meaning the way in which her old hair person would cut one side
of her head short, several times very short. She had probably used an
electric shaver to cut part of her head, rather than scissors. MP and I
were hearing the word "shave" to mean two different things. She
understood, I think, using the tool to cut it very short. I understood
"shave" to mean what Sean and Owen do to their head.

MP got extremely angry with me when I said, "She didn't actually
'**shave**' your head." MP said very angrily, "I was there. I know what
she was doing to me." At the time, either explicitly or via my
intuition, I thought that she was projecting Doug, estranged and
bullying husband, onto me. It wasn't the first time I felt that, and not
just when she confused our names, "Dale" "Doug". "Shave" became a
"trigger", a "catalyst", a "precipitate".

As I learned yesterday, "shave" was yet one more subject we couldn't
talk about. One thing I have come to appreciate more fully since my last
visit in Charlotte, is that the alienation between MP and me is greater
than I understood. The things we "can't talk about" have grown and
grown.

# Presidential debate

I am grateful that Biden seemed to hold his own. He didn't seem to do
something that would undermine his position. I'm not a rabid Democrat.
In fact I have so much given up on political solutions that my fear is
that I am just cynical. In fact, it may be that my decision-making will
be more difficult if Trump loses -- depending on how the rest of the
voting goes. But the fact is that the elevation Amy Barret with the SC
and the prospects of walking back the rights of women, LGBTQ, enforcing
the authoritarian power of the Executive, loss of health insurance as a
human right, these are huge issues.

energy that looks to leaving the country.

else. Ah, my dear.
