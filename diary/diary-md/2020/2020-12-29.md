# The relationship resumes

MP returned home. I tried to keep the power balance. Good for the goose
good for the gander. She tried maintaining "I" language. We both slip
into "you ..." Her boundary: "I will not stay in the same room that I am
being called names, etc." My boundary: "It's not ok with me when the
escalation happens to e.g. 'The marriage is over.' Or 'This is it.'"
That's the key that set me off to not back down last night.
