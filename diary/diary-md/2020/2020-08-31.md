# Cheap grace

"Cheap grace is the preaching of forgiveness without requiring
repentance, baptism without church discipline, Communion without
confession, absolution without personal confession. Cheap grace is grace
without discipleship, grace without the cross, grace without Jesus
Christ, living and incarnate. --*Dietrich Bonhoeffer*"

# Why not "family?"

In the midst of nap or somewhere, and after hearing Mary Pat seemingly
really strike a note against me for not treating Sean as family when I
did with so many others, I realized that there was a distinguishing
characteristic -- a striking one!

In none of the other relationships was one of us in a long-term
relationship with the person. Mary Pat has a 42 year relationship with
Sean.
