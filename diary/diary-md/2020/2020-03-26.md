# Rule of Benedict & Ladder of humility {#Rule of Benedict & Ladder of humility}

Give us this day:

Hence, brethren, if we wish to reach the very highest point of humility
and to arrive speedily at that heavenly exaltation to which ascent is
made through the humility of this present life, we must by our ascending
actions erect the ladder Jacob saw in his dream, on which Angels
appeared to him descending and ascending. By that descent and ascent we
must surely understand nothing else than this, that we descend by
self-exaltation and ascend by humility. And the ladder thus set up is
our life in the world, which the Lord raises up to heaven if our heart
is humbled. For we call our body and soul the sides of the ladder, and
into these sides our divine vocation has inserted the different steps of
humility and discipline we must climb. \[*Rule* ch. 7\]

# Possible assignment for writing {#Possible assignment for writing}

The \"On Being\" podcast I listened to was about a person who recently
published a book, *Delight*. Ross Gay *Tending Joy and Practicing
Delight*. It was so inspiring for me.

The book was born out of a pledge to himself on a birthday that each day
for a year, until his next birthday, he would write about what had
\"delighted\" him on that particular day.

I thought, \"I could do that.\" The discipline about writing I can well
relate to. Vary the assignment? As I walked it occurred to me that a
variation for me is the concept of knowing what we don\'t know. It is in
the blanks. I thought of Leonard Cohen\'s \"the crack is for letting the
light in.\" What surprised me? What do I need to know about? What is
still something that I don\'t know but want to know?

What am I blind to in myself?

Could I do that?
