# Sleeplessness

Sat Oct 17, 2020 04:21 AM

I'm up in the wee hours again. I have been going to sleep a little
earlier the last few nights because I have done so before Mary Pat. But
not this much.

When my dreams are so filled with Hawai'i diocese stuff, it just gets
more and more wound up as time goes on. I know there's no return to
sleep.

Tonight there was the familiar Eucharist where I was presiding, but
there was one issue after another, it had to with corralling others, but
when Bp. Chang saw how late I was, he became angry. Somehow I was acting
on his behalf. The last I checked I was set to begin ca. 45 min. late.
It had some to do with Covid19.

There were various faces on my heart: Kumu K? from Epiphany, now
returned and riding high; there was a mention of the New York state
priest who did so well at St. Mark's. When he imposed **rules** on me at
the Centennial, rather than doing the o'hana thing, I knew that things
were changing in Hawai'i. It's strange that +Bob thinks that it is
returning to "indigenous" leadership -- David G. too. As much as
anything I see LA style high-power stuff as much as anything.

But the point of it all, really, is that I'm out of it. I made a
decision not to be a part of the virtual convention because I had a
sense that it would be too painful for me. Similar to my dream last
night.

My "place in the world". I don't have a place in the old world any more.
Who I was doesn't count any more. That's at least a part of what's
emerged from 2 sessions with Linda in Charlotte.

Viz. a viz. MP, she seems to want, after 48 hours, for things to go back
to normal. She has said, "I love you", several times, however
cautiously, looking to see how I respond. I respond hesitantly. Not that
I don't love, but things seem different now. I'm a "fucking liar".

It was better to just not interact with Sean yesterday. That's why the
going into the bedroom before he got home at ca. 9:30pm. I left a note
with my directions in the cabinet with plastic containers. Trying to
meet my boundary concerns without creating confrontation.

I made some headway with the recovery of the deleted partition on the
old computer. I still can't see any files, but "gparted" thinks the data
is there. It took all day. Solving puzzles. And what does it mean? MP
thinks I'm just distracted and spending time "on the computer".

Her decision about not speaking means we don't talk about important
stuff. Similar to the dynamic at David St. in Ohio. "We love each other.
That's who we are." I don't put much stock in it because it ignores the
serious hurt done. I haven't responded to Shirley's birthday greeting.
Is that my resentment? I guess it is. I guess to find a clear path to a
"peaceful end" I need to seek out a relief from those things.

Siddhartha on the river. Hurts abound. Loss is great indeed. Silent. But
there is clearly peace, shalom.
