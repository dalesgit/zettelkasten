# Sean the decision

I woke reflecting on the "discernment" assignment from Linda. What
decision? Does it have to do with boundaries and limits with Sean? As
the weeks have rolled on with the "no talk zone" in place, I feel more
and more a sense of **resignation** about Sean. I'm not happy about it
but there's nothing I can do about it kine.

-   I'm appalled that our lives are so much controlled by him and his
    tastes and schedule. If it weren't for Sean we could have gone to
    Hilton Head, even if it meant going without children or guests. Mary
    Pat could have visited her mother and family.
-   What Mary Pat turns on the TV depends on whether Sean will be a part
    of it. He refuses to watch *This is us* which so invigorates us.
    "It's not his thing."
