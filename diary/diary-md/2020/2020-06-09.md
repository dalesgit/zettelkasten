# Dream {#Dream}

I wake this morning feeling the unfathomable wound, the jagged
emptiness, caused by children ripped from my life. \"It\'s not about
me\" they say. Emma, Bridget\'s evil culpability, my own powerlessness,
these are the stuff of the dream I slept with in the early morning
hours.

Miriam is doing such miraculous parenting and human coping with the
cataclysm rocking the nation. Her post that MP shared with me from
Facebook where she shared the amazing creativity and sensitivity she is
giving her children, in her way trying to compensate for her own white
privilege.

But in my aching insides I wonder how she can be complicit or unmoved by
condoning the monstrous crime that Bridget perpetrated. Men, fathers,
expendable. William Styron accused of monstrous racism after 1968.

Life can be so cruel.
