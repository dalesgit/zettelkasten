# Morning

20201001220943 Sleep ok. Dreams don't hang onto me like a grown up bully
or a toddler weight. I'm aware of feeling disgust at the presence of
Sean's naked body slouched in my living room. I'm aware of judgment and
projection with the awareness of his brooding smoking right outside my
door. I'm reminded of Billy's presence at mom's house on Venus Dr. I
rehearse the line, "I didn't sign up for this. This is not how I want to

presence whom I don't like or approve of. More than that I don't trust
