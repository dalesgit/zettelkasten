# Something about silences

In my dream there was a combination of software kind of issues and a
systematic effort to inject silences. There was something about how I
was going about it, some kind of trick. There were different keys in the
silences. They were somehow subversive. I was having trouble with it.

I looked across in the bed to MP sleeping. Far apart now. I, at least,
feel a great distance with Sean in the puzzle. She addressed us as,
"Does anybody ..." And I felt so touchy about being grouped with Sean
that my response was, "I'm not anybody." She thought that was
over-sensitive or something and made fun of it. But she addressed me as
Dale.

# Melded psyches?

I think one of the things going on with me these past months is the
following. The boundaries between MP and me were blurred. Others
(Christina) may have seen it as a strong healthy marriage. Perhaps at
some level. But it was precarious by other measures. When Sean appeared,
his mother took on much of his psyche. Then there were 3. I never
bargained for that. I was increasingly uncomfortable with the way in
which my emotions, thoughts, world views were being subsumed or
overlooked. I knew I was going to have to be better at carving out my
own time and space -- e.g. the tv watching.

Sean has accelerated all that.
