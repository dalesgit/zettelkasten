# Psalm 31

^9^ Be gracious to me, O Lord, for I am in distress; My eye is wasted
away from grief, my soul and my body also.

^10^ For my life is spent with sorrow And my years with sighing; My
strength has failed because of my iniquity, And my body has wasted away.

^11^ Because of all my adversaries, I have become a reproach, Especially
to my neighbors, And an object of dread to my acquaintances; Those who
see me in the street flee from me.

^12^ I am forgotten as a dead man, out of mind; I am like a broken
vessel.

[common prayer psalm](http://commonprayer.net/11/06#more-2457) is
dramatic and overwrought, but I identified with it, as I have woke this
morning asking myself how could Miriam go to the wedding and not let me
know? I shared with MP that it is a similar feeling to reckoning with
the fact that a huge swath of the country thinks that racism is ok,
mockery of handicapped, etc.

As I read the psalm I wondered whether I should send it to Miriam? Emma?
To what end? What do I seek from the two of them?
