# 9/11 anniversary

We've watched several videos. It has been a long time since I have been
so immersed in the anniversary. The feelings are a strange mixture.
Clearly a life-changing time. The country never the same. Yet I said to
MP that I thought that the nation has been more damaged by Donald Trump
than by the terrorists on that day. My life has changed so much in the
intervening 20 years. There was an article today and MP mentioned that
far more have died from Covid than did that day. What I had in mind was
that in the last 4 years more of the principles that make us who we are
as a nation have been destroyed. We remember how George W. brought us
together as a nation for the 1st month or so. Then his murderous,
treacherous attacks on Iraq began to be put into place.

Even more profoundly I sense that just as my years are decreasing, human
life on the planet is counting down. If Lydia and Emma are resentful at
what I passed onto them, maybe it's understandable in the global
perspective. If only they could know the depth of love I tried to pass
on to them. But I failed.
