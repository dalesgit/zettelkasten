# Write fool

Our life has utterly changed since S arrival. MP shops with him. Talks
with him. Doesn't talk with me. Spends up to 4 hours a day driving him
to where he needs to be. He decides how the table is set. He was the
first to use the vacuum. He was the first to use the washing machine. He
decides my part of the grocery list. He goes to the hardware store for
me. He takes it on himself that doing dishes and taking the trash are
his jobs. He decides that we have to look at his ½ naked body when he is
in our presence. He pontificates his wisdom.

Like the tragic star of "A Star is Born" Sean has it all together. He
knows.

Sean has no "skin in the game." He trashes a pan MP has had for 20
years. He doesn't know about it. She doesn't care. He is given:

-   room
-   board
-   health ins
-   dental work
-   as much food as he wants
-   comfortable house
-   media consumption
-   driven to work
-   driven to meetings
-   no need for car insurance

His exit strategy is, "Someday I want to have my own business. In 9
months, maybe, I will have a drivers' license and I can buy a car and
drive myself."

# The point

I have lost hope. I said last night in the brief conversation with MP
that I saw **no way out**. Like Becket's "No exit". Like trapped. Stuck
in: US, SC, Rock Hill, living with Sean until I die, gone the church
work, gone church connection (more Jewish than Christian?) Gone
teaching.

So, some of it is retirement. Some of it is getting old. Some of it is
being stuck in a shit-hole country and state. Some of it is longing for
mountains and ocean.

What is the Sean part? He disgusts me. What to do about that?
