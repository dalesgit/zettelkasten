# Owen {#Owen}

I dreamed several times in different ways about Owen. He was my first
born. I preached about first born sons. Owen was the one who opened up
my tears. Joy, but I guess also tears.

I have thought for many years that his plague was similar to mine with
my father. He wasn\'t going to be able to sustain his haughty
superiority. I have not heard from him for a long time now. No thank you
as we gave him \$9,000 for his children. Something\'s going on. Sins of
the fathers?
