# Best teacher {#Best teacher}

## Teachers {#Teachers}

-   4th grade straight A\'s :: It only made an impression later in life
    when I learned that it had caused a major conflict with my sister
    who was also in the class.
-   Beulah Adams :: We made fun of her. She taught me an essential skill
    related to language.
-   High School History :: One short unit on \"Shot heard round the
    world\" and I learned that learning was not about facts and data but
    about inquiry.
-   Senior English (Honors) :: Learned that better grades came with
    using *Cliff Notes* and that learning came with struggling.
-   Harvey Rabin :: ethics & morality, foundation in Greek language
-   Joseph Pickle :: concern for me, mix pastoral with academic,
    *Mr. God, my name is Anna*, *Mr. Blue*, *Diary \... of*, *Red &
    Black*, (what else in that introductory class?) But then we read
    Kierkegaard together.
-   Piano teachers :: Language skill, trivium & quadrivium, 7 liberal
    arts. \'Before they became known by their Latin variations (artes
    liberales, septem artes liberales, studia liberalia),\[3\] the
    liberal arts were the continuation of Ancient Greek methods of
    enquiry that began with a \"desire for a universal
    understanding.\"\'
-   Tom Deeds :: colleague, but one I followed wherever he went
-   Terry Milligan :: like Tom, but my peer. He was interested in
    \[\]{#continual learning}**continual learning** like me. cf. report
    5/14. Terry was like a spiritual twin.
-   Louis Weil :: principle of oldest liturgical practice (name?). Laws
    against celibacy are a way of dating the practice.
-   Bart DeMerchant :: hi-lo Anglican vs. belief in a living God.
-   (NH Librarian) :: knowing only 4 things
-   Stanley Hauerwas :: brutal honesty
-   NH Ethics professor :: Kierkegaard and Buddhism, \"crazy\"?
-   Patrisha Wismer :: gave me a taste of teaching
-   John Dunne :: spirituality \-- all is mysticism

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

-   telling me incisive things I remember
-   many I didn\'t connect very well with, but they gave me something
    that has fed me for a life: language, basic research tools,
-   Most of them I didn\'t think of as \*[]{#friends}**friends**\*, but
    all as somehow \"fellow citizens\"

## Impact on my life {#Impact on my life}

-   never forgotten, lasting impact
-   Left me with a conviction, \"I want to be like that.\"
-   They gave me a roadmap of artists, authors, ... I wanted to know
    better.
-   They inspired to pursue something, knowledge, vocation,...
