# Waking dream

Sean had done some kind of wacky manic thing like he has done in the
past. I can't remember what it was. Now I do. He had boxed up a bunch of
our books (More than we have at home right now) and put them into huge
boxes that were placed into the entrance way of our house. I remember
some philosophy books in the bunch, books I haven't had since the 80's.

Like the painting of the apartment 18 years ago, actions that I have
only known through MP stories, it was a mess that would take a lot of
work and energy to clean up. Most of all it was an indication to me
that, "OK, Sean's in a manic phase now."

Does the dream indicate my own sense of having lost myself and seeking
to find it again? "Finding my place in the cosmos" I had said to Linda.
It's a huge metaphorical task. The couple's person -- Janet -- doesn't
deal with subtlety, but I was determined to get something positive out
of the interview, I was determined not to be identified as the "loser",
and I think she was plodding but determined to get a "task" for me/us to
be working on and that the task would be built from things I had
identified or said.

Talk about boundaries. Maybe I can't sit in the living room with him for
long periods of time. Dinner is problematic. Perhaps a boundary is that
I need to step up and determine my own diet. A la Olga-Maria and Amy
with their eating issues.
