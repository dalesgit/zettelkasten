# Counseling Day 2

Sleep seemed comfortable, but I wondered in the middle of the night
about that as Mary Pat grabbed my hand as I slept. It pulled me out of
whatever dreaming I was in. I felt comfortable in my sleep but jarred
toward waking with her hand. I wondered if I had been groaning or
something, maybe snoring?

Perhaps meeting with Erin can be a time of clarifying in my own mind and
heart what's going on.

It's Sean. It's Mary Pat. It's that I'm unhappy. It's the relationship,
surely changed because now there's three.

I thought of the particular irritation I felt earlier when she would
lump Sean and me together as "her guys." When she wanted me to be Sean's
buddy. To go kayaking with him. When she swaps us out as the kitchen
cleaning help.

There's not room for 3 in the kitchen.

On Tuesday at Clericus Zoom, Sally wondered how or when I could go back
to Hawai'i (for a visit). I described what I knew, but the terrible
truth for me is that there is no going back. Talking with my student on
Zoom yesterday. She was there with the military in 1970. She observed on
her own that, "It's different whether you are visiting or living in
Hawai'i."

I can't go back to living in Hawai'i, except under extraordinary
circumstances.

Saying that makes me realize there's another trigger point for me. All
the countless times that Mary Pat has responded to the question "Why did
you leave Hawai'i." She has only given her reasons. My reason was
always, "I'm going where you're going." It was simple for me. Having
Sean here was similar. I said, "I don't see we have a choice but to
say,'Yes'." But I think I always thought it was going to be problematic,
and I think I said, virtually from the beginning, that we have to
establish boundaries.

And from the beginning I felt unheard. Royal "we" has never included me.
It was always, "We were there for 12 years," when I was there for 13.

Lydia and Emma still both think of Hawai'i as "home." It surprised me to
hear that from Lydia a few months ago when asking her about her
Christmas plans. I suppose it will be so for them as long as Bridget is
there. But maybe longer because it was so formative.

I think it was so for me as well.
