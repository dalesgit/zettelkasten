# Awe-shucks

Dreaming in a setting with the atmosphere of "Oklahoma" -- a kind of
"awe-shucks" sort of thing. There wasn't a frustration theme like
yesterday. Just gatherings. Seemed like families. But more than that,
community. After the dream I thought Mary Pat didn't seem to be around,
but I think she was there, somewhere.

I slept pretty well, waking often to pee. There's a certain sort of
cease-fire sense of peace, I guess. We can't talk about what's important
to me. But I forgave her, I said so, anyway, for her injurious words --
she apologized after praying together yesterday -- and it seemed good to
spend time together -- a long day with Sean at work, watching a movie.
But ...

Two days of effort were attached to working at solving computer puzzles.
I wanted to try the "Elive" distro. It's supposed to be the best
performing with the least overhead. But in trying to install it, which
ran into difficulties, the old "Home" partition was deleted. At first
nothing booted. Then I got it to boot. With old Antix drives I got the
partition to show up, then the data showed up with gparted, then I
figured out how to restore files to an old usb. The partitioning is
screwed up, swap partition gone, but "Elive" is bootable and I have
access to all the files I should want.

Elive does appear both beautiful and lean. Why is it that the browsers
take up so much CPU as compared to 10 years ago. It so quickly goes to
100%. Otherwise the old computer might be usable.

But wait, it might be useful as a networked harddrive -- even without a
screen, keyboard etc. I would have some 60-80 gigs available.
Interesting thought.
