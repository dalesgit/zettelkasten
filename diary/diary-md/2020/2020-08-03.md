# Sue Monk Kidd's journey

I have just finished her memoir of the 90's, *When the heart waits*. I
ended it, leafing through the notes, thinking, "This is an account of
me. If I had been a writer, this is what I would have written. Certainly
these are the authors I was reading and the writers who impacted me the
most."

Yesterday I finished her latest novel, *The Book of Longings*, imagining
the wife of Jesus, representing the divine feminine. Again, I recognized
the importance of the *Nag Hammadi* texts in her writing the novel, the
feminine voice, "Little Thunder" the epithet Jesus used of her, and the
text from the collection of "The Thunder Perfect Mind":

I am the first and the last\
I am she who is honored and she who is mocked\
I am the whore and the holy woman...

Now I am looking at beginning the earlier memoir, about discovering
God's **love**, *God's joyful surprise*. Looking at the notes, here too
I will find much that is familiar by way of nostalgia. "If I had been a
writer ...". But there is no point in turning away from the **sacred
now**.
