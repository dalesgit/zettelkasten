# In the end the day collapsed

MP wanted to go to bed early. I think she was angry at me. Don't know
why. Maybe she didn't think I stirred the soup appropriately. Maybe she
wanted me to volunteer to go get Sean because she repeated several times
that she was "so tired."

After supper we watched the show with a Syrian refugee doctor. Then she
announced that she was going to bed, asking me what I was going to do. I
said I'd be in shortly. Sean announced that he was going to watch some
show he wanted to see. I announced that I would be leaving the living
room, they both knew that it was in disgust.

I came to bed and almost finished the book *Why? the Holocaust?*
Depressing stuff. And unbending and principled in a way, but
overthrowing some assumptions of the modern world, but leaving things at
a heavy existentialist sort of decision-point.

That's what I feel. I don't think the marriage is going to make it.
Because MP will always choose her son over me. And I don't think I can
make it until he leaves. I can't abide his attitude of privilege and
entitlement ... (deservingness)? which is fostered by what I think is
correctly named by Linda MP's codependency.
