# Dream last night {#Dream last night}

Tue Jan 28, 2020 07:34 AM

I was in a place like Colorado Springs. There was family around but I
was alone. I had set out on some task. I had driven the white Kia. It
was a familiar route, but somehow changed or I wasn\'t remembering well.
I parked somewhere and walked and walked. Maybe ran a bit. Maybe some
other mobility. Did what I needed, I think, more or less. But then
trying to get back to where I needed to be. Mary Pat was somewhere
north. Somewhere other than where I was. I couldn\'t quite remember
where the car was. I tried and tried. But I couldn\'t remember where I
parked it. I couldn\'t get back.

It seems as if some friendly folks had taken me, perhaps out of the
weather. And finally I called Mary Pat. And she just kind of laughed and
conveyed to me that it was my problem and I\'d have to deal with it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

We had watched *The Notebook* and MP had wondered how much of the
Alzheimer theme Bill and Karla were actually aware of. I thought they
were, but by the end I thought perhaps it was too close to home for them
to have enjoyed.

Bill & Karla and I had gone to Jamestown without MP. She was sick and
the walking would have been too severe. Then with the preparation for
dinner (leftovers), the 3 of us were doing part of the task and it felt
like MP was sitting alone, allowing herself to be waited on. Then we
didn\'t do it the way she wanted. Then I lost it because I didn\'t know
all of what was going on. I told her she needed to get involved. Her
response was to walk away and attempt to leave me on my own.
