# Watching election returns

I can't watch. Tue Nov 03, 2020 11:03 PM. CNN is laboriously following
the same pattern as in 2016.

Blessed text exchange with Lydia, recalling her jubilation at the
election of Obama in 2008. She has an ugly premonition of doom for this
election.

I said I share it, while still hoping. By tomorrow will the hope be
gone? They've been saying for days that it may be days and weeks before
we know. But a Biden victory might be predictable if enough states flip.
That doesn't seem to be happening right now.

I told Lydia that I had thought about moving to **France**, **Canada**,
or **Portugal** if Trump wins. I still may decide enough is enough.
Where do I want to live out my days? I think it's possible I will decide
not here.

A place where a huge swath of the population:

-   thinks it's OK to bully
-   thinks that thumbing our nose at the rest of the world is a good
    thing for the US
-   that psychologically flawed human being is suitable to lead us (ala
    Nero?)
-   ...

And on it goes. It would, it would seem, be without Mary Pat. So that's
a big decision. But a lot is riding on the joint counseling session next
week.

I didn't like sitting in the living room with Sean preparing for his
driver's license exam which apparently he is going to take tomorrow
morning. I wasn't informed about it, of course.
