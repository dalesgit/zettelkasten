### Insight (due: 2020-10-09)

It seems clear to me today, mid-day, that the real felt injury for me is
that **I have been shut out of decision-making with re. Sean**.

I would also acknowledge that I share a part of the responsibility for
that because I abdicated input, largely because I thought that the
parent relationship trumped anything the step-parent thought.

In retrospect it seems that I experienced the primary relationship to be
the marriage and that I needed to be more a part of deliberations.

However, I don't think the pattern we had would allow -- and it
certainly doesn't permit now -- that I have anything to contribute. Mary
Pat's decisions are final.

There is a pattern of her responses to my thoughts is to explain why
they are wrong. She doesn't recognize that pattern.

All along these months, it seems to me, while she made final decisions,
the question could have been asked, "How will Dale's concerns be
addressed?"

So the "forgiveness" is directed at Mary Pat.
