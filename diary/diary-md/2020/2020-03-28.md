# The crack in bowl? {#The crack in bowl?}

Perhaps it is the issue of wanting and choosing. I spent a lot of my
life doing what I \"ought\" to do, thus sacrificing who I was. Pleasing
my father, etc. But \"choosing\" something and being okay with it seems
equally powerful to me. It feels like all of the great acts of
generosity and sacrifice are based on that. Jäggerstätter, Jesus, Henry
Nouwen. Each of them found their []{#joy}**joy** in doing what they
chose. Maybe that\'s it. Finding my []{#joy}**joy**.

Ross Gay was claiming \"delight\" for his life. From the movie last
night, *Good Will Hunting*, we saw a heart-warming love story \--
heart-breaking too. Will chose in the end to see what would happen with
\"a girl\". He didn\'t know the answer but he accepted the concept that
he had to be all in to be able to find out. I thought repeatedly last
night that that\'s what I had done with Mary Pat.
