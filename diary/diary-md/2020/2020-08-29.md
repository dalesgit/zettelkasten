# Richard Rohr's meditation today

#grief He recommends the "Welcoming prayer", which, of course, I am
familiar with through centering prayer.

I realized I could pray that for the anger and grief I feel with regard
to Sean's intrusion into my/our life.

> After you can identify the hurt and feel it in your body, welcome it.
> Stop fighting it. Stop splitting and blaming. Welcome the grief.
> Welcome the anger. It's hard to do, but for some reason, when we name
> it, feel it, and welcome it, transformation can begin.

> ... When you're able to welcome your own pain, you will, in some way,
> feel the pain of the whole world. This is what it means to be
> human---and, also, what it means to be divine.
