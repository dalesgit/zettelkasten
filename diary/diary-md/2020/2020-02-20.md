You shall not exploit your neighbor. You shall not commit robbery. You
shall not withhold overnight the wages of your laborer. You shall not
insult the deaf, or put a stumbling block in front of the blind, but you

partiality to the weak nor deference to the mighty, but judge your
neighbor justly. You shall not go about spreading slander among your
people; nor shall you stand by idly when your neighbor's life is at
stake. I am the Lord.
