August 16, 2020

Last night intense dreaming. There was pastoral ministry in Hawai'i.
Then rehearsing thoughts about my relationship with Mary Pat. I don't
know if any of it was healthy.

I thought that I stay quiet, refrain from much conversation because when
I say something, it is either:

1.  Interrupted before it is finished or treated with dismissive
    feeling, "Oh," before moving on to something "important."
2.  Negating or arguing with whatever I said.

Yesterday I realized another element that evokes Bridget. MP expects me
to be stupid or abusive in what I say. My words are filtered through
such a lens. That came out when she described her immediate response to
me in the exchange about her hair. She was saying that her hair dresser
had shaved her head. She asked the question, "When did she do that?" and
tried to sort it out. I started saying, "Well, she didn't exactly shave
it and it went through various cuts every 3 weeks." Her response was
based on a strong gut response along the lines, "What does he think he's
saying? I was there. I know that she took a razor to my head. How dare
he speak for me."

In processing this, I tried to say that having the thought, "How could
he say that?" is a clue that she could pause and ask, "What does he
mean?" In this case, it is really only after a night's sleep that I
realize the diffence was in language use. She said "shave" and meant
that her barber had used a razor or shaver to cut her hair. I understood
"shave" to mean that it was the process that Sean and Owen have used on
their head and men use on their face. I spoke largely because Sean
wasn't there and couldn't have known exactly what her cut looked like.
