# Dreaming of Hawaii {#Dreaming of Hawaii}

It was somehow a little different. But it was home. With similar
tensions to what I feel about going to Monroe to preach and facing the
sabotaging student on Monday.

We cleaned up Christmas yesterday and vacuumed a lot of dust. Mary Pat
fixed lovely lamb for dinner and we drank the champagne that had been in
the refrigerator for ages. Watched *New York, New York*. The song was
composed for Liza M. and this movie \-- not Frank Sinatra. The two of
them occasionally sang it in duet.
