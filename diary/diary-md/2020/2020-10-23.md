I went to sleep having played for a few moments with the Markdown
software "Obsidian". It is very intriguing, though sadly not open
source. It allows for re-visualizing a folder of markdown files.

In my dream, evidently, the upcoming encounter of Hawai'i in tomorrow's
convention was on my heart. I was in Hawai'i. Following a basic pattern
that I've had for a long time, I was wondering in a church oriented
place. There was a lot that seemed abandoned or re-purposed. There were
gatherings of Christians I sort of recognized. But I felt disconnected
from them. And I felt OK about it.Not in the sense that I was at peace
with a separation, but that I am identifying less and less with the
Episcopal church.

The lectures I am finishing about the early modern Reformation has
affected me. The lecturer is obviously no friend of Catholicism or
Anglicanism, but he matter-of-factly claims that the Elizabethan
settlement was in fact a Protestant move, just one with nuance and
calculated political appeal.

The Protestants he describes across many lectures are indeed plagued
with a virus that seems to be in the DNA, one of division and rancor and
self-grandiosity. And the violence, present on all sides, of
Christianity in the 16th-17th centuries is breathtaking.
