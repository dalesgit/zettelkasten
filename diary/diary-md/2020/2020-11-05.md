# Dream on awakening

I was somehow with a young woman. We were both trying to get somewhere.
Did we have to catch a plane? We were in a place that was a mélange of
places I've known. We were both a part of something, like we were both
Episcopal priests or something. But we wanted to get in a run before we
got to our destination. There was traffic and congestion, routes to
figure out. We headed somewhere but then got picked up by a car with
pastry family to give us a lift. We got out and headed out for our run
when I awoke.

I didn't have equipment. I was going to have to run in my bare feet. I
anticipated the route up ahead where there was a rocky, mountainous
area. I thought I can do this. She was better equipped and was up ahead
of me.

After awakening I wondered, "Who was the woman?" I had no emotional
investment in her at all. I wondered if it was anticipating my session
today with Linda S.? She did prompt some significant self-examination
last month. I'm grateful. I haven't got better, but I think I've made a
good beginning.

The blow that Emma was married last week was difficult emotionally
yesterday. I feel: hurt, angry, hopeless about family affairs, wondering
to God "Why? After the huge investment in loving that I made in those
girls", suspicious of my family who won't tell me why Emma is so cut
off.

Mary Pat had said yesterday, "Emma doesn't look like any of the rest of
your kids." I responded yes, I've known that a long time. They other 2
girls are not very connected to me, but they're not utterly alienated
the way Emma is.

The deeper question I have is something like, "What did I do wrong?" But
it's too late in life to correct the behavior. Is it too late in life
for a reconciliation? How would I do that?
