# Covid-19 and the church {#Covid-19 and the church}

Sam Rodman has produced a policy yesterday for the diocese that is
pretty far-reaching. The only troubling part I felt had to do with his
call that the clergy not participate in \"common cup\" as well as laity.
I didn\'t know if he meant there would be no chalice on the altar. And
if so how was the consecrated wine to be handled?

-   Consecrated elements should be handled reverently. I still remember
    Bart DeMerchant\'s story about seeing an altar guild somewhere in
    the country taking a loaf of consecrated bread and dumping it in the
    trash.
-   A principle for me since seminary has been that I don\'t
    []{#pretend}**pretend** at anything during the liturgy. If there\'s
    no water in the cruet \-- don\'t pretend there is. If there\'s no
    bread and wine \-- you go get some.

For now Charlotte is ok with consuming the small amount of wine in
chalice as I revest the chalice and paten. If we move to removing it to
the sacristy I want there to be safe-guards that make sure it is handled
and consumed reverently.

She is going to take care of notices to the congregation and seeing
about the possibility of a notice in the bulletin.

She reports that the ongoing saga of the funeral has been quite
something and that I should be glad I couldn\'t do it.
