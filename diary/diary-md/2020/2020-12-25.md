# Yesterday at Great Falls

It was the end of an era. First time Speedy was not at a service. It
ended up being just Cindy and Hank, who had together gone to a great
deal of trouble to make the church suitable for worship. The last
recorded service had been March 1. Outwardly, by the usual standards
(count) it was sad.

But it was so beautiful. The music and contemplative atmosphere we
sustained for an hour was the best ever for me at that place. That it
was two siblings brought together for their rapidly declining parents
was focused and they were receptive. My message was that we are
especially aware of our great need right now, the need for which the
Incarnation was the response. They both were aware. My words were
somehow just right.

Cindy has been in Greenville since Thanksgiving and will be staying
through December, away from her family. Hank is off work for a few
weeks, carrying the burden of Great Falls, perhaps estranged from his
wife, the immediate person watching his world fade away.

I was really glad to have been there.

Mary Pat had prepared a fondue supper for us. Especially Sean was the
touchstone for her, but it was appropriate for me as well, or perhaps my
heart was a little softened or just especially flexible for Christmas.
