# Dream {#Dream}

but we were just at the beginning. We were meeting up with other parts
of the family. Which? Who? I don't know. But we were in land similar to
Colorado.

an attack from a foreign government. There was an ocean and ships and
they were being bombed, similar to Pearl Harbor. We tried to tell people
about it, but they ignored us and went on their way. What perplexed me
was the bit about no one paying attention, or caring, or believing the
news. It seems like what is going on now with Trump.
