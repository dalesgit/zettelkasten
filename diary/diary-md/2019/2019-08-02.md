# Dream {#Dream}

I had four of my children with me, somehow two of them were close, the
other two more distant. We were visiting in an unfamiliar town. there
were mountains and pine forests. Something a little like Manitou
Springs? I had traveled the territory via bicycle before. what was our
destination? I'm not sure. We got sidetracked or lost and found
ourselves in the driveway of an important person. I think it was the
governor or maybe the mayor who was returning -- perhaps from Church
services. Christmas?

I made a point of greeting them and saying I wanted them to meet my
family. This was an attempt to defuse their anger at our having squatted
in their driveway. At this point, though, there were 2 cars. I went to
the first and didn't know who they were. The mayor assumed one was my
mother, but I had to say I didn't know. But upon introducing
themselves, it seemed that perhaps they were in-laws. Then we moved to
our car. At this point my mother was in it, but I don't remember her
being their before.

Everything was good.

Today Amy arrives and the over-full weekend begins with 2 concerts and
then hooking up with Gary.
