# Impasse with MP {#Impasse with MP}

Things were tentative from yesterday. Kind of left in limbo. Today went
ok until I returned from the errand trip she sent me on. I walked in and
started talking about the interview I was listening to on the radio --
with Margaret Atwood about her just released novel. MP said she didn't
hear me as I was putting down the groceries. I turned and went back
outside to get the 2nd batch of bags. She said to me, "I guess you
didn't get my text." I felt exasperated. I said that she had told me
that earlier, but that I had bought a few extra things. She said, "OK"
in a tone that seemed to convey to me a kind of weary acceptance. At
some point I said it's frustrating hearing the first words that come to
me a litany of the things I did wrong. I must have said that with some
feeling. Perhaps my voice raised a little. But we were talking to one
another in different rooms and she had said that she couldn't hear me.

As I was unpacking the groceries she said something like, "I didn't
hear you said." I repeated in some fashion what I have written, raising
my voice by some volume. What feels like "raising my voice" is felt by
her to be anger and she reacts very strongly to it.

In the bedroom where she had retreated, I followed her and said in a
somewhat raised voice what I had experienced. I said I wasn't angry.
She said, "Your voice is angry." I said it's the same volume as you
left me with.

She finished with something to the effect that, "Your anger is too
much." I responded with, "Your passive aggression is too much."

# Trying to process {#Trying to process}

## Observations {#Observations}

-   It does no good trying to psycho-analyze what's going on in her.
    Been there. Done that.
-   My anger. I have thought it stemmed from depression and tried
    several times to convey that to MP. I have never felt that sharing
    with her was productive. My further assumption has been that MP's
    volatility and repeated negativity -- toward me and just about
    everybody else -- was the result of the same thing.
-   I have regularly felt that she interprets some responses from me as
    anger when they are not. I've asked myself why is she prone to do
    that? Sensitivity or fear of anger? But I think that's probably a
    fruitless conjecture.
-   I am easily convinced that there's nothing I can do about where she
    is.
-   I have felt the need to be in a place where it was OK to be angry,
    one of the basic emotions. What is abundantly clear to me is that my
    love for her transcends any of the emotions.
