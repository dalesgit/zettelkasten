# All I want for Christmas {#All I want for Christmas}

We had an exchange yester even after we got home. "What would you like
for Christmas?" She said a few things. I thought for a while. It was
difficult. But I wanted to take the question seriously.

-   The first thing I said was I wanted to be able to draw. Ever since I
    was little I wanted to have talent for it. But I didn't.
-   The next thing, similar, I want to be able to play piano. Of course
    in one sense I've been able to play my whole life. But I've wanted
    to be able to play it like a musician. Cf. my current struggle to
    transcribe *These Islands*.
-   The last thing I said, a simple acquisition kine, was another pair
    of jeans. Made of stuff that is sort of comfortable to wear.

I included:

-   a storm door like the Dayton house has
-   a fireplace that works
-   live by the ocean
-   to be able to talk to my daughters
