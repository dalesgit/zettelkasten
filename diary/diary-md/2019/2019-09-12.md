# Everyone was there {#Everyone was there}

Dream this morn where all my everyone seemed to be present. Even
Shirley. We were going to an event of some kind. Waiting for everyone to
there before we headed out. Bill was one of the leaders, but not the
only one. Karla, Julian, there were handicapped (or recovering people)
-- was it Mary Pat? Going to a church, sort of. Also like going to a
sports event, with concourses etc.

The thing that stands out is that I wasn't being hindered from getting
where we needed to go. I woke in the midst of heading up one of the
concourses, sort of herding all the people.

# Mary Pat {#Mary Pat}

Mary Pat and I slept in separate beds, she remained in the guest
bedroom. But we ate my supper, though separately because she said it was
"so cold" in the living room. Weird. But we embraced in the kitchen
and expressed our love. It was almost even erotic for me. But so, so
welcome. Then she went her separate way.
