# Dinner with Peter & Christina {#Dinner with Peter & Christina}

Trying to talk heady stuff with Peter felt like it didn't go very far.
He mostly was eager to talk about his work. My arguing from my
perspective didn't seem to connect. I had too many
[]{#parentheses}**parentheses** in so that he didn't follow. I made
reference to my concern that Elizabeth Schussler-Fiorenza was too often
arguing from silence. Peter just looked at me not understanding and not
following.

Cf. the principles of feminist criticism from the OT text:

-   looking at unexamined women's roles
-   looking for the censored and unspoken part of the text
-   changing the interpretative thrust of the text so that later
    generations can do better than the exploitation that past
    generations have done

Cf. also the text *Sarah, Hagar, and their children* where all the
authors are feminist theologians: Christian, Jewish, & Muslim.

What is interesting is my seeming need to stretch my thinking, even as
MP was feeling left out. All too often I am feeling the same way when
she is controlling the conversation.
