# Opening reflections {#Opening reflections}

Sense of well-being in my sleep. I awaken with what seems like an odd
thought, that I feel lonely. It's connected with a statement yesterday
MP made to Amy on the phone, that we would most likely move to Florida.
And I had the thought -- or a feeling really -- that I would like to
have family around. Maybe it's something like Lydia's deep sense of
needing to move to where her sense of "family" was, i.e. South Bend. I
don't think it's such a great thing for her, nor would it be for me, I
think, and yet ...

Mary Pat feels the same thing, and yet she's the one who moved away
from family early on and has never looked back. I remarked yesterday
that I have been in Orlando 4 times, but it's not a place I want to
live in. But either of the 2 places we've stopped at previously I could
be satisfied with.

I miss being in a place where a liberal or progressive perspective is
dominant, and I certainly couldn't live in the same environment that
Judy lives in: she has a concealed carry permit, is an outdoorsy kind of
woman who fits with the Florida country culture that is, I think, deeply
"red."
