# Moving {#Moving}

Yesterday Mary Pat made reference in conversation with someone on the
trip to the Everglades that "when we move to Florida ..." I acted a
little surprised and said, "We haven't had that discussion." She
responded, saying, "I thought we had. We were going to buy a house in
Palm Harbor ..."

I know that sometimes she imagines that things have been discussed and
settled. I know that she often says, "I thought that ..." whatever it
was that she was predisposed to believe. I often think on those
occasions that she doesn't listen to me at all.

I got up a little early because I was just going over and over in my
head the notion that I was about to move to Florida.

1.  I always thought of South Carolina as a temporary stay.
2.  I just got through getting back my role as a teacher and Mary Pat
    has made the decision to move.
3.  None of my concerns with regard to dealing with our house are taken
    seriously: refrigerator, door, carpet, garage door.
4.  I know she does want to please me, but she is often dismissive of
    me, ignoring my talking, pointedly interrupting me in conversations
    so that others know []{#who's in charge.}**who's in charge.**
5.  Sometimes I feel like everything I say, from minor to major, she
    will refute or start off with how I'm wrong or not quite right. She
    will presume that I am asking her to []{#fix it}**fix it**.
