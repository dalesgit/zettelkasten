# Fruit of dreaming or lying in half-sleep {#Fruit of dreaming or lying in half-sleep}

Fear of dying? I think not as much as I am afraid of losing the cutting
edge of self-reflection. That's the importance of this little piece.

I wondered if I should move back to using a physical book and a pen.
It's slower but has a long history.

Fatty liver disease. I don't really know what it means. I know that
quitting drinking alcohol is an obvious step that should be taken.
"What makes life worth living?"

Thinking about the Jeff Bezos piece about doing away with presentations
in []{#meetings.}**meetings.** Instead reading a short essay and then
framing a discussion around guided questions. I have had classes like
that -- and I think some of the best.

Size of the class will determine whether I can manage class discussions
or they have to be smaller groups.

I miss Lydia and Emma. Such rich memories -- built pretty deeply on
love. And just thrown away.

And Bridget? To have no relationship? I wish only to have something for
the sake of the children. But there is nothing. I guess that was the
failure of all those years of "doing it for the children."

The profound cynicism and violence of the Trump years really weighs on
me. Britain is going through the last throws of the ending of their
glory. Greece was once a glorious place and passed on a glorious
heritage. Now it is struggles to avoid being a failed state and for many
centuries wasn't even that.Now it's Britain's turn to fade into the
background. Trump has ushered in the era for the US. Putin is reigning
through the glory-years of the Soviet experiment. China even newer at
playing master of the universe (earth).

You can do so much more when you play the []{#long game}**long game**.
Palestinians. Chinese. So why not Africans? Indigenous peoples? Women?
Homosexuals? Let them rise up.

Do I hear a melody? Is it some kind of wisdom?
