# Dream {#Dream}

Dream that seemed to go on and on before I woke just before my alarm. It
was a church setting. There were new policies, a new vision, being
implemented. It had to do with being an inclusive church. Clearly they
were successful, but there still seemed to be a challenge at the core.
There were two parts to the new policies, one part a radical statement
and the second a kind of enumeration of specific situations.

Perhaps I was influenced by the *New Amsterdam* episode we watched last
night where the couple had a child who received a heart transplant. As a
family they were unable to afford the tremendous cost of the medications
he needed to take to keep from rejecting the new heart. The solution the
hospital found was to get a divorce (on paper) and the mother and the
child would go on medicare so that the government would pay for the
meds. The family objected because they were strict catholics.

Also an influence on the dream was our anticipated visit with the
St. Paul's, Monroe this morning. Old friends. Good people. But Kathleen
is a follower of Pope Benedict.

Also, it seemed that in my dream I (we?) had young children. There was a
kind of camping experience going on. The youngest child seemed to be
someone like Julian. Bill talked about seeing Julian as well as Owen on
their trip to Colorado. They are leaving today to drive home. Bill
seemed possibly interested in flying out here for a visit and then to
perhaps Williamsburg.
