# Prayer {#Prayer}

What do I know about prayer? But I am preaching on it on Sunday. So
humility is my background. I am just a beginner. Cf. Thomas Merton's
prayer: I think I want what you want, Lord, but the opportunities of
self-deception are everywhere and all the time.

# Bill again {#Bill again}

Last night Bill asked me whether I remembered that Dad had got custody
of David at some point. He gave me hints. And I seemed to recollect a
change in circumstances when David left St. John's and returned to
Denver. He had 2 years of football and success at a Lutheran High
School. That was the time period and it led me to think about what was
going on in my life then that I had so little recollection.

Of course to begin with I'm now almost 70 and it was a long time ago in
human years. But, with some calculations, I would have been about 27
years old. I was in the process of entering postulancy and beginning to
think about going to seminary.

# David called on Facetime {#David called on Facetime}

I really liked again being able to see the Bill, Karla and David. I
asked David if he ever had 2<sup>nd</sub> thoughts about not making
a career of the Navy. He quickly responded that "only all the time."
But, he went on, he wouldn't have met T. It sounded so much like my
reflections about Bridget. I so wish she had not been a part of my life.
But without her Lydia and Emma would not have been, and even with the
loss I feel with regard to each of them, I would trade the realities.
