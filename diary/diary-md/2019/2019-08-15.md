# Dreaming {#Dreaming}

I was doing church work. I was working with groups. Maybe a little the
evening with Barb, Sue, and Olga-Maria. They each had their own pastoral
universe. Mary Pat had been in charge there. There were, in the dream,

work was a message about what my primary focus ought to be in
retirement. Not academia, but the church. I have been cavalier in many
ways, not paying attention to the political aspects of the Episcopal
Church, dismissing them too easily perhaps. But it has been very good to
write them off as in the larger scheme of things not being very
important.
