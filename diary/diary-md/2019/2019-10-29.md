Yesterday the trolley and soaking in St. Augustine. For me the best was
the Castillo, built in the 1600's. But there was a sense of weird
combination of historical roots combined with a tacky tourist pitch.
Henry Flagler, partner of John D. Rockefeller, built the foundations of
the tourist pitch with a series of grand hotels as he built up this
destination as an anchor for his train that rain down to the Miami area
and then the keys.

I was so glad when Julian borrowed the bike and went on what would be a
long ride (but for him a short one) to the []{#Dollar Tree}**Dollar
Tree** and the post office. Miriam communicated her new address. The
memories continue to flow.
