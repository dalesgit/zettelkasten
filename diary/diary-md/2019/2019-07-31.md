# Dream {#Dream}

Shirley and I were interacting at a family reunion. I don't know what
family, because there were lots of people there. Tens if not a hundred
or so. It was in Colorado, I imagine, because there were mountains and
pine forests. Shirley was somehow humbled and therefore approachable. Or
perhaps I had forgiven her for the great destruction she had caused. We
weren't friends but we talked. It all seemed important in the dream,
but now I can't remember very much.

We watched episodes from *New Amsterdam* last night. We are both caught
up in and care about the characters. MP and I both agree that one of the
female actors is beautiful. I pointed it out because most of the time I
don't have a sense of beauty other than inner beauty shining out.
