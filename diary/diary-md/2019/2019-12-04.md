Where oh where are we. MP reported that she spent most of her time at
the doctor's office crying. They were profoundly concerned about her
depression and sought to connect her with counselor -- at "Saluda".
MP asked me if I would go for marriage counseling there and I said yes.

respond in part because she doesn't say directly what she means. I
guess it goes with the territory. I should be better.

She said she's []{#so alone}**so alone** because she used to have the
best partnership with me, but now she feels it slipping away. She
regards my mood swings as a sign of behavior change that reminds her of
what was reported about her dad prior to his first major heart attack.

least as "my choice" that somehow I could undo. I think she wants me
to be unencumbered, no teaching, no commitment to churches.

Here I am, finally, talking about her -- just the way I did for so many
years with Bridget. That's the awful part. And I don't know what to do
about myself under those circumstances.

Jesus." My insight was that "the coming of Jesus" ought not to be
thought of in terms of the coming at Christmas or the coming in 2019 but
rather as "the 2nd coming" of Jesus.

[]{#anamnesis}**anamnesis** of retelling the story, the experience of

understanding of time, indeed the "*homo religiosus*" of Eliade.
