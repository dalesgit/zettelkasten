Since I finished my Susan Howatch novel -- via the 6th in the series,
*Absolute Truths* -- I'm not sure what to pick up next. The novel
allowed me to turn on a little of the self-reflection that the series
originally did. I remember telling Bob Fitzpatrick about the novels, and

these things were addressed in the series.

But perhaps the James Martin piece on the Jesuit way of life could lead
