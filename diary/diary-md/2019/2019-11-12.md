Over and over again I witness it.

-   unnamed couple in Michigan City who hadn't formally gone through a
    divorce. The widow returned and flexed her power for the sake of the
    money
-   Mary Jean being offended and demanding others listen that my mother
    discreetly came to mourn her former husband's death (my dad)
-   Bridget's sister and her, "You don't have the right to speak
    here." - Shirley foisting her views on all comers at her own
    mother's funeral
-   Beth manipulating her sister out of the care-giver's picture

Is it people demanding that no one infringe on their particular
grieving? Is it demanding the right to dictate how other people may
grieve? Is it refusing to allow others to grieve? Is it greed? Is it
power?
