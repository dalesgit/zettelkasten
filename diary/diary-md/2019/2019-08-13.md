The seven "Oh".

70 is a big number but it's not quite prime. Ah, there's more. It's a
round number that isn't thereby hip. Looked at from below, it is a
scary number. But quite tame from the vantage of, say, 75 or 80.

By age 70 one doesn't hold a lot of stock in birthdays, But your mother
would scoff at thinking it old at all. The age one *[]{#is}**is*** and
the age one *[]{#feels}**feels*** are not the same. Just See me when I
totter out of bed at night.

70 gives us lots of space to flow in and out, Like the clouds flowing
past a hot-air balloon; Experience undergirding our presence, While
wonder carries us deeper than Crater Lake.

Our children have reached an age that seemed out of reach When we were
young. Wisdom remains as elusive as then. I would love to own the rhythm
to dance the liturgy, But your arm in mine is sufficient for ever and
always.

That hat I gave you as you gave me a flapper-smile, lit your face and
smote my heart And never more shall I not love you or Stanch the pull of
just to look you in the eyes.

What? you ask. Just to see you is my satisfaction. We make a dance to
the melody of life, in a two-step shuffle Or just arm in arm, doing our
best. "On the lips", you tell me, pointer finger on your mouth.
