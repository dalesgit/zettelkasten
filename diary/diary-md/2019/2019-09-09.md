# Alienation {#Alienation}

Dreaming I was in the mountains, foothills. I was doing a lot of running
(physical). I'd been a part of some kind of conference or retreat.
There was an issue getting into and out of the complex. I had betrayed
the person I was with. I was trying to make it better. To repair the
break. I couldn't. I had used Mary Pat's Miata but I had lost it. I
couldn't find it. I was frantic.

Reflecting a little the day yesterday that was filled with alienation.
Things that set me off included MP saying, "I shouldn't have told you
that." It reflects a self censorship that I somehow associate with the
brokeness of life with Bridget. I was reliving that part of my life the
other day as I tried to write for the sake of posterity so that the
children would someday know.

She said she wanted to have a normal conversation but that everything
she said seemed to be the wrong thing. I was so sad because that was the
same thing I wanted. Was it like what Bridget and I had when things were
so broken? I can't remember.

I tried to share my pain and struggle with regard to: Winthrop, Rock
Hill. She didn't know how to respond. She said that she had thought
that when I returned the key to Peter that I would be telling him that I
would not be teaching. I didn't know how to answer when she said that
if I decided to teach that it was OK with her but that she was done. But
then she imagined a situation where she would be involved with 1 hour of
Jane's spring schedule. It seems clear to me that she doesn't really
want me to teach, but she doesn't exactly say so.

I just hate the not talking. But one of my thoughts about the teaching
was that I think we shouldn't be spending 24/7 together, that just the
normal course of things we would get on one another's nerves.

Emptying my office yesterday felt genuine in the sense that without MP
at Winthrop I didn't really have a place. My "standing" there was
very minimalist -- a mere adjunct who was more disposable than the
lowest rung of employee. The same applied to Winthrop. The feeling led
me to thinking that I would relish moving. I would be glad to set out
for Naples. But MP didn't know how to respond. I told her that I was
making recommendations but just trying to share how I felt. Later she
recalled that I had told her I didn't want to hear what she had to say.
I couldn't remember the exact words, but I said that I never would tell
her that I didn't want to hear. But I guess in the context when I said
I wasn't talking about doing something -- to which she could respond,
"That's a good idea" or "That's a bad idea" or "I/We should do x,
y, z" -- but rather trying to share what's going on inside me.

She's really not very good at dealing with that.
