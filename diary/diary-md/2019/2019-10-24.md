Walking the edge of the ocean, occasionally rocked by the force of the
wave remnant, sinking in the sand, the cool water now warm on my skin, I
could begin to rest in a little bit of self-reflection. I wondered,
[]{#Why do I get up in the morning? What am I living for?}**Why do I
get up in the morning? What am I living for?** Obviously shades of
Mom's reflections, but closer to home I thought about how I looked
intently into a future trajectory as I walked the beach in Hawai'i. At
Mokule'ia. On Molokai with the bishop candidates. Waikiki, even with
Mary Pat by my side. But now there seems to be a lot of blank.
