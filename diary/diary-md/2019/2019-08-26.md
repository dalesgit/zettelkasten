# Yesterday {#Yesterday}

I was a real poop on the way to the party at Bob's and at the party
itself. Driving, I passed my phone to MP, expecting her to tell me which
of the choices I had for River on her birthday she liked. Instead she
imagined I was asking her to make the purchase, which she ultimately
did, on my phone, that was not well set up for making the purchase.

Later at Bob's house she said I ought to get in touch with Miriam to
see if she was going to be there to receive the package. Miriam finally
responded saying that they were at Ring Lake Ranch. While waiting for
that MP said I needed to reschedule the package if possible. That meant
canceling the order. I ended up sending Miriam a message that I would
resend the package when she let me know when they would be home.

I imagine there are hurt feelings all around.

Then, I want to reflect a little on how depressing the party felt to me.
I just don't know why. I think first of all, of course, about the
changes in me. I'm not going back to teaching. I've been struggling
with the retirement thing. But with some reflection I think there's
more to it. Late in the party I learned that Kirsten Heider is pregnant
again. They are expecting their 4th in 5 years. It felt depressing to
me. Driving back I said to MP that "the news did not feel celebratory
to me." I couldn't encompass all the feelings. But I felt anger at
Kevin for inflicting that on Kristen. I felt alienation from the sense
that it was a right-wing Catholic acting too much "in your face."

The one thought I had throughout sitting on the lanai next to the pool,
watching the energy of all the young "next generation", playing in the
pool with their new-fashioned "swimmies", that I was bewildered and
somehow lost with trying to grasp what kind of world those little ones
will grow up into. I felt bewildered at the mentality and inner-life of
the parents of the babies, wondering what their expectation of the
future of the planet and our country was.

Finally, I think I felt depressed (anger?) about the fact that I am cut
off from the life of my grandchildren. My choices, many of them.
Today's reality in some ways. The reality that families tend to be
defined by the mother. In Lydia's case I think maybe Mama Sue and Didi
-- to use her childhood names for them.
