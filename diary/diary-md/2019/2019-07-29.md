# Confrontation {#Confrontation}

Mary Pat was wrapping up odds and ends with the papers strewn around the
guest room. There was a reference to the 2 plastic boxes of files in the
office and she asked, "Is there anything in there that ought to go in
the filing cabinet?" I didn't know how to answer, thinking about the
hours that I had spent discarding things and consolidating down to those
two boxes. My hesitation alone -- perhaps there was a look on my face
also -- set her to throwing up her hands and walking away.

We had a talk where she projected an assumption that my files were
something I was keeping from her and I told her that she could look at
any of the files and make a decision about whether they needed to go in
the filing cabinet. We both expressed frustration and not being
appreciated. I began to tell her about my perception was that there was
her way alone.
