# Irony {#Irony}

I told MP at some point today, "Actually, practically everything I say
is at least tinged with irony."

That was an important statement for me to process. I think it in fact is
largely true.

It came in the context of MP and I talking a little bit after she
expressed alarm at my anger expressed at Dale Westerbrook intruding in
our tree work -- after all the preparatory work I invested and in
effect calling a halt to work on "his trees." What I had to say about
that is that I wish there was clarity about who owned the trees. I then
had stomped away proclaiming that the work was not something for the HOA
but was simply []{#maintenance}**maintenance**.

Both of us expressed the obvious truth that we are both under a lot of
pressure. The unspoken recommendation I would give to a couple coming to
me is to have patience and flexibility with your partner.

No irony with that recommendation.
