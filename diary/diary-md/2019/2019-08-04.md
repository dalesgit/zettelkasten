Stayed up very late in order to be able to drive Patch's truck down to
venue to pick up her equipment. Once when I dropped it off and again
during the concert some middle-aged men expressed to me their real
admiration for Amy. It was wonderful to be able to lift her up and to

The band is sort of losing its guitarist, however, as he is moving to
Nashville to advance his career.

# Boomers source of all that's wrong {#Boomers source of all that's wrong}

At a certain point in conversation with Amy I light-heartedly made the
statement (mock fashion addressed to Mary Pat), "Of course it's the
boomers who are the source of all that's wrong with the world."

What surprised me was that Amy responded without any irony in her face
at all that, "Yes, that's true."

I thought of Owen and his convictions about Libertarianism, the fall of
the American Empire, his effort to be unlike his father at all costs.

Then I tried to reflect on how much can be blamed on our generation, the
generation that, even as we are moving out of the echelons of power and
influence, we have been at the forefront of just about everything in the
US -- and through its influence -- the rest of the world.
