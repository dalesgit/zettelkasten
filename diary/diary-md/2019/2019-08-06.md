# Dream(s) {#Dream(s)}

Early in my sleep I had an old dream. I was in charge of the liturgy. It
was relatively important. But I messed up everything about it. It was a
complete and utter failure.

Prior to waking another more common and recent dream. Similar theme I
guess.

> Could it be related to accepting a late invitation to supply at the
Chapel of Christ the King in Charlotte this coming Sunday?

I was supposed to play a part in a large graduation ceremony. There were
several people with me. Perhaps Julian? (He called). Or a boy and a
girl? We were shunted off somewhere to wait for a period. But then I
missed the group I was supposed to be with -- handing out the diplomas
or some such thing. Then I was madly racing around trying to catch up
with my group or at least find out what I was supposed to do. I failed.
