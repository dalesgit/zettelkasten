We have arrived in Naples. The sun is rising over water as I look out
the lanai window. It is finally comfortable, mostly feeling like we are
home. We are just the two of us. We are not staying at someone's house
that I don't really know. Martha and Mike were friendly enough, but
whether it was Mike's seeming intense shyness or Martha's OCD, they
seemed uncomfortable with these two strangers in their midst, even
though Martha and Mary Pat are cousins.

We came fairly close to buying a house in their neck of the woods. At
least both MP and I backed away from it, just on prudence grounds. We
learned, though, that we don't really have enough financial resources
to buy a second home without really stretching our money reserves.

We celebrating my dinner at a strip mall restaurant, "The Mystic
Fish", that was actually a very nice place with good food,
well-presented. I over tipped by accident. Today it is church in a very
affluent Episcopal congregation.

# Dabney Smith {#Dabney Smith}

I bring you a voice from your past. Dale Hathaway, at one time from
St. Andrew's by the Lake in Michigan City. I spent 13 years in Hawai'i
and retired from parish ministry in 2012. Since that time I have been
teaching in several universities, the last of which is in South
Carolina. My wife has just retired from teaching and she and I are
spending October in Florida. We may be moving here in the not too
distant future.

I wanted to get in touch with you while we were visiting, but my reason
for writing has snuck up on me. We went to church this morning at
Trinity by the Cove in Naples. In quite a few ways it was an awesome
morning and I was impressed by the breadth of ministry going on there.
The organ was beautiful and the sermon was -- well, my wife turned to
me and said, "When did you write that sermon." I chided the preacher
(Zimmerman) that his name must be Habbakuk. (He didn't get it.)

So, here's my question. Are all the churches in your diocese like
Trinity? Jocularity aside, if some possibility presents itself, I would
value having conversation with you. Currently I am supplying as often as
I choose to in the dioceses of North Carolina and Upper South Carolina.
We live in interesting times.

Aloha and Shalom, Dale C. Hathaway +
