# Home, with dysfunction {#Home, with dysfunction}

So I wake up this morning before 6, quite unable to sleep. We are home
together for the first time since the end of September. MP's sisters
have cast her out of their midst -- it's a system vomiting out the
alien dynamic that doesn't fit their way of doing things. That's what
I was thinking about when I decided to just go ahead and get up. It'
"Katy's house." They needed the "eggs" that she laid for them. I
was mistaken in my appraisal of the family and probably helped to set
her up for the terrible disappointment she had.

I really despise the manner that most families, apparently, -- maybe
it's just some, but ... -- the way families respond at the time of
death.

Deception. Inability to tell the truth or to be transparent.
Manipulation. Willingness to hurt others who have a right to be a part
of the grieving process.

Can I get back to a sense of being "me"?

needing scheduling and decisions: the garage door, the trees,

something about your cataract. It really scared me when you said that
you couldn't see."

I was so frustrated. Being given an ultimatum. She asked what do you
want? (or some such.)

I said, "Well, the only thing that seemed attractive was to go (to
