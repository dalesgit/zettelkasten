# Depression {#Depression}

There's a lethargy about my spirit that feels persistent. It's not
debilitating but always just around the corner. Where is the future to
fight for? What am I getting up for in the morning? I feel so much like
I'm walking in mother's shoes, her last 10 years or so.

What to do about it? Certainly activate the silver sneakers benefit I
have. One value of the YMCA is that it would be indoors, out of the
oppressive weather.

# Bill & Miriam {#Bill & Miriam}

In the phone call of two days ago [2019-07-24](2019-07-24.html) Bill
reported that Miriam had contacted him after a long period of silence.
She apologized and then asked him for an estimate of how much they might
expect from the sale of the house. Her urgency she explained with an
account of how 2 doors down from them a former inmate had moved in. His
crime had been drug (heroin) addiction.

My intuition about Miriam's silence toward Bill as well as possibly me
is that she has had a growing sense of []{#existential
fear}**existential fear**. Is it a result of inner changes going on
inside her? Is it responses to growing alienation from Lee? Fear of what
is happening in the country? I don't know.
