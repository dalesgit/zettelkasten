# Relg 101 today {#Relg 101 today}

Challenge to address the conviction of "multiple truths". Some get the
idea that knowledge is somehow based on evidence or logic. The notion
that religion is of the category of []{#opinion}**opinion** runs deep.

I emphasized with the second class that it was helpful to them to at
least know where I am coming from.

At least 1 person recognized that moving from opinion to knowledge one
moved from weaker to stronger.

I emphasized in both classes the recommendation not to use the phrases
"I believe that ..." or "I feel that ..."
