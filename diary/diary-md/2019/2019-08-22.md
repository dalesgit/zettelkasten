#dream 
I awoke with a start to realize that I had been dreaming intensely about
parish priest kinds of things. I was in the office of the new rector of
St. Mary's. I had been involved with conversations about some technical
things related to the computer network and internet connection there.
Talking with Leland? or just referring to Leland and then referring to
Chris.

It was strange that the school had been kicked out and the church
expanded to all the space. The rector seemed a nice guy but unaware of
the history. Sort of clueless.

It was perhaps related to a brief exchange in the car driving to Dayton.
I made mention of the fact that I had been with meetings at the very
beginning of organizing Family Promise in Hawai'i. Mary Pat seemed
surprised. It took me back to memories of when I was a respectable and
admired rector in east Honolulu. One of the "senior" rectors.

It was all a little bit similar to the feelings I had talking with
Gordon and Janey -- but mostly with Janey. I make a good kind of
senior, silent, partner in ministry with her. Or perhaps it's just that
the memories carry me back to earlier days.

Talking about my interest in Nicolas Tesla at such a young age. Making
the model of Edison's bulb. I immediately then identified with Tesla.
And I realized that my identification with the counter-cultural type
goes back a long ways. It surprised me.

Last night as I was in the bathroom, MP came up to ask about whether I
wanted to watch a movie. She observed that her mother had changed into
jammies in the living room. When they asked her, "What if Dale came
in?" she answered that she didn't care and I wouldn't care. I felt so
honored.
