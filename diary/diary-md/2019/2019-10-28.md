Yesterday we drove to Anastasia State Park. Julian and I rode bicycles
for 2 hours while Mary Pat napped and scouted restaurants. She picked
one out that we had dinner at, sitting outside and watching the sun go
down over the inland waterway. Seafood platter, shecrab soup, low
country boil. Wonderful dinner.

At the park Julian and I rode around every little trail, walked out to
the water, across the dunes, and saw the site of the quarry from which
the "coquina stone" was dug by prisoner, Native American, and slave
labor to build the fort that we're planning to see today.
