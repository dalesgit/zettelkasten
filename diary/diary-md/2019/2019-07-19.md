# Journal {#Journal .justcenter}

I slept well. I had taken Mucinex expectorant and it really seemed to
believe that the thick mucus was better.

We had dinner with Sue Parker. Among other things she conveyed to me
that I was her model for how to preach. I feel humbled. I don't really
know why she feels that way -- obviously lots of other folks don't
feel as strongly as she. But it made me think of the way I really poured
my heart out for the folks at the Chapel on Sunday.

Today, perhaps we will find out a little more about Mary Pat's
situation with Social Security. Perhaps not yet enough.
