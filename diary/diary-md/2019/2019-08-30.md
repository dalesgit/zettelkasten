# Prayer group {#Prayer group}

## Sue, Barb, Olga-Maria, Mary Pat, {#Sue, Barb, Olga-Maria, Mary Pat,}

This is an update-report on the prayer-group meeting yesterday that was
attended by Mary Pat, Olga-Maria, and myself. As we had talked about at
the meeting at our house a few weeks ago, the purpose for yesterday's
meeting was to decide on some of the specifics for going forward with
the group. Our time together at the WELCM house was prayerful and I
think grace-full. We came to several conclusions about the immediate
future of the group:

-   The place and time for the meeting will be the WELCM house, 4:30 pm
    on Thursdays.
-   The format will be a simplified lectio or African Bible study
    method.
-   the meeting will be open to students and hopefully it will become a
    part of the student's "programming".

One of the reasons for re-imagining the group as a part of the
programming for the campus ministry was that we would need the
ministry's governing board's approval to meet there. We each had an
intuitive sense that it was "appropriate" at several levels, that a
kind of "radical hospitality" was at the center of all prayer and
especially prayer groups, and that we all grow from relationships that
are outside our norms.

The next meeting will be 4:30 pm, Sept. 5 at WELCM house.

## Separate to Sue {#Separate to Sue}

Sue I am sending out a notice to each of us involved with the prayer
group, reporting on the decisions that our conversation yesterday
brought us to. I'm sending you this note because you had voiced
concerns about the concept of inviting students from campus ministry to
be a part of the prayer group.

Each of the three of us yesterday sensed a clear convergence of vision
around something happening at the WELCM location. Olga-Maria is
experiencing right now an expansion of that ministry in various ways
that will be unfolding over the coming weeks and months. She gave a
powerful witness to the ministry there being one of offering what she
called "radical hospitality." In the note to the whole group I try to
identify several points of our further reasoning.

Most of all I wanted you to have a heads up so you could pray about and
reflect on the meaning of the prayer group for you. I would very much
welcome conversation with you about it if you would like. I leave that
to you. In the meantime, get well and we'll see you soon.
