## Three Rules {#Three Rules}

WHEN HENRY JAMES, of all people, was saying goodbye once to his young
nephew Billy, his brother William's son, he said something that the boy
never forgot. And of all the labyrinthine and impenetrably subtle things
that that most labyrinthine and impenetrable old romancer could have
said, what he did say was this: "There are three things that are
important in human life. The first is to be kind. The second is to be
kind. The third is to be kind."

-Originally published in *The Clown in the Belfry*
