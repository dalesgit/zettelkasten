# Dream about Lydia {#Dream about Lydia}

It was totally about her although there were others in it. I was driving
around Honolulu with Lydia & Emma and someone else. Julian? Scott? Bill?
Lydia was acting out in some way. Recalcitrant? Depressed? I was telling
this other person that we had lived in Hawai\'i for 3 years. Lydia
wanted to argue with it. Then we were at an intersection. She jumped out
and got into a little plastic car which a grown up clown was driving.
They quickly drove off.

I was panicky. I parked the car as soon as I could. Then was arguing
with police and someone else about where to leave the car. I tried to
explain I had lost my daughter. They didn\'t really listen. I was
distraught.

The clown made me think of Stephen King. The situation was just
obviously malevolent. I got up.
