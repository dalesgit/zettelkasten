# Reading *Zen* {#Reading Zen}

*& the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance* =

It is late. Time to have been in bed for an hour, but I\'m not. I am
getting to know \"Phaedrus\". He is the alter-ego of the narrator. He is
the person the narrator had been before he went insane. He is a thinker.
He struggles to avoid the nihilism which is thought seems to thrust him.
He understands the conversations about \"*a priori*\" and other extended
metaphors. I read them now and remember back to the resonance they had
for me when I was 20 y/o.

## Dreaming {#Dreaming}

There was a kind of normalcy to my dreams. I can\'t actually remember
them now. I have worked for a while. But they weren\'t disturbing
although fantastic in a dream sort of way. Helped perhaps by reading the
book before turning out the lights. I was able to recognize, or at least
remember a little bit, my very young self as a 20 year-old, caught up in
the reveries of the novel and its antagonist? *Phaedrus*.
