# Letter {#Letter}

Susan,

Thank you for the update on your schedule. It sounds a little bit hectic
and crazy. One of my thoughts was your good fortune, certainly blessing,
that you have so much family centered around Madison. Perhaps the most
poignant thing you said was wondering about \"the devil\'s work\" in
leading you to a sense of unworthiness. That seems to me to be possibly
very important and an insight you need to pay attention to. Somebody
long ago, almost as a joke, \"If that\'s true, it\'s important.\" I
think there\'s a lot of that in our lives.

I would love to work with you in reading []{#Romans}**Romans**. I\'m
just a little guy, not like Barth or Luther or many others who found
their truest voice of faith in their interpretation of Romans \--
[]{#but}**but** opening up Romans was a vital part of my adult
conversion experience.

Mary Pat and I had intended to be at Our Savior for your preaching, but
I was called on very short notice to supply in Camden, so that\'s where
we were yesterday. Mary Pat and I are interested in finding a time for
study/prayer. Would Wednesday at 5:30 at Mary Pat\'s office work for
you?

Faithfully, Dale +
