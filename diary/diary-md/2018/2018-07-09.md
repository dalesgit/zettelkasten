# Sunday at the oldest African American Episc. Church in SC {#Sunday at the oldest African American Episc. Church in SC}

I really enjoyed myself. I felt privileged being there. Fear of being an
over-bearing white liberal.

Once I got to the hall it was weird. There were certain trappings of a
not unusual meal sort of reception. But nobody was there! The person I
had talked to, the treasurer was in another room with \-- was it the
Bishop\'s Warden?

Then the whole thing got colored by the fact that I ended up leaving my
vestments there. Grrr!
