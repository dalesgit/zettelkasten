# Good Friday in Monroe {#Good Friday in Monroe}

Our home for 3 days is a motel room. Happily it\'s fairly nice, with a
hide-a-bed and a soft chair and coffee table. My liturgies have been
well-received. Mary Pat said that she felt like crying several times
during today\'s liturgy. I had a sense that there was a solemnity and an
awareness of God\'s presence that was palpable. I\'m grateful.
