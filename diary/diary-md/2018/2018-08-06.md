# Finished Elizabeth Berg\'s *Arthur Truluv* {#Finished Elizabeth Berg's Arthur Truluv}

Perhaps there will be a reconciliation with Mary Patricia. The book made
me cry for the very real sense of love, across generations, and through
a multiplicity of personal narratives and foibles.

I read most of it without glasses. It was easy reading, of course, but
there was a satisfaction in the reading that took me back to earlier
days when I seemed to be able to enter into the books. My
[]{#vision}**vision** for many years, peering through eyeglasses, seemed
to get in the way as much as they facilitated.
