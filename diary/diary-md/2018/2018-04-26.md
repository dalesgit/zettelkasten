# Late night to bed (1am) {#Late night to bed (1am)}

I feel so much venom towards Miriam. I feel it in the effort to evaluate
how I feel about the possibility of breaking off a relationship with her
over her cultivating a separate relationship with Bridget and with
Emmma.

I feel conflicted about that. But I feel equally adamant about the point
I said to her that our intentions don\'t always have the effect that we
want. That there are \"\[\]{#unintended consequences.}**unintended
consequences.**\" I am content at the moment to rest with that
conviction.

Who has won? At one level I am just protecting myself and my primary
relationships. I can\'t continue to walk into a 2-faced relationship
with a naive expectation.

## Being in touch with my anger? {#Being in touch with my anger?}

I wake up an all I can think about is Bob Fitzpatrick. The trigger or
association is the email yesterday about upcoming ordinations. Long
list, 6-7 maybe. Bob has really impacted the diocese of Hawai\'i. Maybe
he\'s solved the problems and challenges. Maybe my anger is simply
resentment that I couldn\'t be a part of the solution. That I was cast
aside as an impediment to Bob having his will. i.e. e.g. he saw major
problems with the \[\]{#Total Ministry}**Total Ministry** solution. He
had a better way. But the ones he has chosen that I knew had real
problems.

-   Malcolm. Had a scandalous lack of knowledge about the Old Testament
    and didn\'t appreciate that it was a gap. He carried a 30 year chip
    on his shoulder, nurtured by a cadre of followers from around the
    globe, wanting to see movement supporting indigenous peoples. I
    couldn\'t fathom the sense I got from him and others that the world
    had to conform to his notion. Bob turned out to be the same way. A
    terrible listener.
-   Paul Lucas. He came before the Standing Committee at an early stage.
    But I had never encountered anyone so consumed with rage and a sense
    of his own self-righteousness.
-   Then there was the early response from the priest on Molokai. She
    needed to go. It was a knotty problem. But her testimony was that
    Bob was a liar. I dismissed the idea. But it turns out that Bob is a
    liar.
-   Bill Mok accused Greg of being a liar. I took umbrage. And yet I
    think that in that case Bill was right.

The point here, being that I need to recognize the depth of anger within
me. Do I want to take it to the grave? I think that Liz Z. maybe carries
that kind of anger. So many rectors Bob forced out, punishing the
congregation along the way.
