# About dreaming {#About dreaming}

I realized something today as I was waking up. That the thing that often
pushes me to wake up is dreaming about some work/task I need to do
during the day, and then dreaming about it over and over again.

I thought that very mundane thought as I was realizing that I had not
been dreaming about Hawai\'i. I had been the last few days. And in my
waking time I had realized that my dreams are often set in Hawai\'i.

Part of my reverie was prompted by several emails from +Bob. I so detest
the man. Yet it is because of him that I ended up in Hawai\'i. I am so
appalled by the fitness of the portrait of him as a malevolent religious
dictator, cf. John\'s conversations and the book he and ? shared about
Michael Servitus. And yet in Bob\'s message to the diocese from
yesterday, describing his take on the spirit of the church in Hawai\'i,
I am supportive of most of it. Where I differ is in a disagreement about
his own view of the church as \"Catholic.\" I thought even while on the
Standing Committee, and often in later months, that he doesn\'t know
himself very well. That he poses thoughts for public consumption but
doesn\'t recognize that is own actions belie his statements. His
self-righteousness is another example.

Ah. So it is.
