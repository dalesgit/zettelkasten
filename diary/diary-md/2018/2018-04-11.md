# Dream in color {#Dream in color}

I awoke briefly while it was still dark. Then falling back into sleep I
was startled, staying alert for a few minutes, by the vivid color of the
dream. I was floating, like a drone, above a very green forest canopy.
There were clouds lazily floating down among and around the trees.

I went on to dream about I think Hawai\'i. At least the feel was like
Hawai\'i. There was a sense of security and love.

I have been taken aback a little by the extraordinary warm response I
got from the people of Our Savior last Sunday. Word spread rapidly that
I was the best supply they\'d ever had \-- and that sort of thing.
Extending to Frank Pollano\'s mother \-- then to Frank \-- and to
Jeannie Haubert in my office area. She confessed to me that they have
worshipped at St. Peter\'s in uptown, but the distance has led them to
start attending Our Savior. She said she wished that they had been there
to hear me \"preach\". She used language that didn\'t sound
Episcopalian.

The build up of enthusiasm around the 2 of us at St. Paul\'s has been
similar. It is true that it is now going to wane quickly as a new rector
has been chosen, but I found myself thinking week by week, \"How can I
match the enthusiasm that was generated last week?\" And I am not really
reaching for the enthusiasm. It is more wanting to be genuine and
competent. Both places appreciated my singing. One person at Our Savior
was especially keen on the closing of my sermon. I have recently been
more focused on the closing. But this person had some words, apparently
commonly known, about what he thought should be there:
