# Dream {#Dream}

Familiar pattern but new details. I was in a drama class. The teacher
was familiar to me and helpful and understanding. Sort of like Gary? Or
Mary Pat\'s Tom Polaski? We had been given a book of scripts a day or
two before with our assignments. I had briefly looked at mine but not
rehearsed it. I had a 1 person play.

We showed up for class and we were effectively doing a performance. I
was last, but in the meantime, people were streaming into the classroom.
They were friends of ours, peers, members of the wider community,
intending to be an audience. Somehow a notice had gone out and there was
a huge response.

It came to my turn and I was frantically just trying to find the pages.
I couldn\'t make it work. The teacher came over to help.

I knew that I could do a commendable job if I could just get the text in
front of me.

I woke.
