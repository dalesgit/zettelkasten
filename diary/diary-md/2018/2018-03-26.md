= Bill called at 7:30 pm ==

He said that Mom was mostly despondent, but she bounced back at one
point that she could eat her lunch. She talked a little with BIll and he
got her to do a little puzzle (3 questions). When he talked to the nurse
she obviously felt like Mom could go at any moment. But then she bounced
back. Bill sounded weary and has decided to stay some more days.

Karla said that she wishes she could be there to help him. I said the
same thing. I felt connected to both of them. Mary Pat observes that if
I weren\'t teaching, I would be down there. That\'s probably true.

I told Bill that now he was going through the dying process. He
remembered that he had been with Mom and George just shortly before he
died, but that he wasn\'t present for the time of death.

I suggested that he raise the topic of []{#hospice}**hospice**. He said
that that sounded like a good idea. It would be better for Mom and would
get her out of the care that is only oriented toward recovery. If he
asks we can get a sense of how the medical folks see it at this point.

In a later call that Mary Pat made to Karla, we heard an amazing account
of mom reaching out to Shirley. Karla reported that, as mom awoke from
one of her stupor and said, \"Oh, is that my Shirley?\"
