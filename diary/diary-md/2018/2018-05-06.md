# Mom\'s funeral: a dream {#Mom's funeral: a dream}

Woke from dream contented and floating toward a good place. A little
bit, I guess, like last night\'s movie \"Places of the Heart.\"

I wanted to put Mom\'s young portrait as a nurse on the cover of the
funeral bulletin. Bill nixed it. He has other ideas \-- and he\'s the
one making decisions. Walking, I listened to Nitty Gritty Dirt Band
playing \"Amazing Grace\" \-- I thought that would meet some of my
requirements for a song to sing. The President led the nation in singing
that! I thought. Their next song about living in a mansion, or making
home where you make it, or love is the only thing that endures \-- these
were candidates for music to accompany the funeral.
