# Insomnia in the night {#Insomnia in the night}

I have a long day ahead of me and still I couldn\'t sleep. Some was
ruminating about +Bob. Some not.

Then it hit me that what I was lacking was a
\*[]{#purpose}**purpose**\*. Just as Mom had found her purpose in the
pigeons, so I have found it along the way: parish ministry, teaching,
preparing the house for moving, etc. The purpose of caring for Mary Pat
is laudable but it has seemed somehow enervating.

I am really thinking of the character Rudolph Abel in the movie we saw
the other night, \[\]{#Bridge of Spies}**Bridge of Spies**. When
repeatedly asked if he was worried, he responded, \"Would it help?\" in
the calmest most Buddhist way.

+Bob is a rascal, []{#koloho}**koloho**, is that right? But he doesn\'t
have peace. I repeated to myself that I have cut out the main things
that I made sure I pursued in Hawai\'i: counsel, walking, writing.
