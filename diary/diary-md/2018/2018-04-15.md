# Return to Monroe {#Return to Monroe}

generated in Monroe and last week at Our Savior. People saying things
like, \"Best ever\" and so on. I am scared I can live up to the
expectations, that the bubble will pop, that I will be exposed for who I
am.

But I think of, e.g. Bishop Steve Charleston (sp?) and his white pony
tail and his humility which combines with a determined pride in his
indigenous heritage.
