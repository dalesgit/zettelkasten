# Strange dream in Vegas {#Strange dream in Vegas}

Such a strange dream to wake up with. The dream had covered territory in
some way whereby I had been an adulterer \-- a cheater \-- with a woman.
Precisely what I did not feel at all with Mary Pat. Then I was with, I
think, Mary Pat, although there a kind of tinge of other women that I
have known. I was happy with Mary Pat.

Then the final episode. We had a car and we had somewhere we needed to
be. We were in an urban kind of place \-- a kind of mix of Lakewood,
growing up, and Vegas which is outside our window. I left Mary Pat
somewhere to go get the car and pick her up. While doing that I got
lost. I was almost there when I pulled into a parking lot to get my
bearings. Suddenly something came flying in through the window to land
on my lap as I sat behind the steering wheel. I frantically tried to
identify it and then get rid of it. It was a []{#pig}**pig**! I threw it
onto the street. Then someone came up, knocking on my window. It seemed
like he was the owner of the little grocery store I was parked near.
With my engine running, obviously wanting to leave, he asked who I was.
I told him my name and he said, \"OK and walked away.\" At that point a
woman came running up to pick up the pig and hugged and kissed him like
an injured child of the family. Then I woke up.
