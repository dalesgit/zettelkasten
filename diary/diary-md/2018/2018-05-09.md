# Mass at Our Savior (kept Ascension Day) {#Mass at Our Savior (kept Ascension Day)}

I spent some time putting together a few themes surrounding Ascension.
My idea was to do overview of that and then share my response from first
Father and then Mother after death, revealing that the message in both
cases was \"I\'m ok.\" Then to invite the listeners to share as called.

As seems to have happened over and over again, the people listening to
me here in this neck of the woods seem to be blown away by my ministry
with them. \"You are such a breath of fresh air,\" I have heard over and
over again.

I\'m humbled. I don\'t think it\'s really me doing it, though I
recognize the freedom I feel in being retired. I\'m grateful. It\'s the
Lord doing the work.
