# closing day at Winthrop {#closing day at Winthrop}

Last class in Modern Christian Thought. I have thought that I really let
the class get away from me. It had top notch students and very
under-performing students. How to get both to have a positive
experience? I don\'t really feel good about what I have provided. I
thought of this when Peter talked last night about his capstone projects
with majors. They sound extraordinary. But it\'s not a fair comparison,
of course.

Peter also seemed to be \"courting me\" as he talked about the
possibility of providing students a \"pre-seminary\" track at WU. \"We
could use an Episcopalian.\" Mary Pat said later that she thinks he
would really like to have me on the faculty. That if things had gone
differently I might have gone on to finish the Ph.D. and that she would
continue teaching if I was doing something that energized me.

I will be said, actually, to not be teaching. Even though I experience a
lot of stress from it. I would really like to stay engaged somehow. In
fact, I suppose, blogging and YouTube allow for some possibility along
those lines.

Perhaps developing an online presence that I felt good about?

## Talk with Bill {#Talk with Bill}

He reports that Mom now has an MRSA infection, presumably from the
catheter. Other things, kidney, are doing better. Bill & Karla are now
planning on going down next week. But he has to get better. Not
responding to the antibiotics.

He points to the support that Karla can give him by being there. She\'s
up to it.

I was taken by his talk about the occasion of Barbara Bush dying.
Somehow I had been aware of the same age of the 2 women. Bill had
listened to news about George W. going to say good bye to his mom.
Bill\'s really wrestling with the heavy stuff.

valley of death. He said that \"she appreciated it.\" I\'m not really
sure what he was referring to. I\'m thinking that it was my trip down
before first of year.

I couldn\'t tell if maybe he was urging me to decide to go down now. Or
maybe to accompany him. Just kind of a weird exchange. But big time
stuff.
