# 20 of the Most Popular Sandwiches From All Over the World

> ## Excerpt
> These sandwich recipes feature global flavors. Whether inspired by or fixtures of cuisines and cultures all around the world, these dishes are a great way to send your taste buds on a journey over lunch break.

---
One of the major draws of world travel is getting a taste of various [cuisines](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipes/86/world-cuisine/). But even if you don't have any trips planned, you can still send your taste buds abroad—over lunch break! Consider the [sandwich](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipes/251/main-dish/sandwiches/): a universal food item, but one that can look and taste vastly different from place to place. Check out some of our favorite top-rated sandwich recipes from around the world. Best of all, they're simple to make at home, using [ingredients](https://www.allrecipes.com/gallery/budget-friendly-dinners-using-pantry-ingredients/) you can easily find at the grocery store. So now you can thrill to the flavors of the world, one lunch at a time.

## Torta Milanesa de Pollo

Buckwheat Queen

Inspired by the Mexican dish cotoletta alla Milanese, this sandwich is served on a bun and dressed with a spicy mayo spread, avocado, lettuce, tomato, and pickled vegetables.

## Pan Bagnat (Pressed French Tuna Sandwich)

Chef John

This French-style tuna sandwich is akin to Niçoise, the famous tuna salad, stuffed and pressed inside a whole loaf of bread. For optimum flavor, make it ahead to allow the juices to meld, marinate, and brighten for a delicious meal.

## Shrimp Po' Boys

Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

Louisiana's signature sandwich, the po' boys can come stuffed with anything from fried catfish to ham and cheese. Fried Shrimp Po' Boys, however, might be the most popular of all.

## Korean Street Toast

Chef John

This Korean-inspired street toast recipe is a slightly sweet, super savory vegetable and egg patty is sandwiched with smoky ham, melty Cheddar, and an amalgam of mayo and ketchup between two slices of crispy, buttered bread.

## Roasted Pork Banh Mi (Vietnamese Sandwich)

Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

Chef John explains the brilliance of this Vietnamese sandwich: "Not only do we get amazing contrasts in flavor and texture, but also the temperature difference between the crisp, warm, meat-filled roll and the cool, crunchy vegetables makes this so much fun to eat. This isn't just one of the best sandwiches I've ever had, it's one of the best things, period."

## Classic Cuban Midnight (Medianoche) Sandwich

DOTDASH MEREDITH FOOD STUDIOS 

"This is one of the more famous sandwiches straight out of the island of Cuba," says Pretty Pink Bullets. Load thinly-sliced ham, pork, Swiss cheese, and dill pickle slices into sweet rolls and heat in a sandwich press for crispy, melty perfection.

## Japanese Egg Salad Sandwich (Tamago Sando)

Japanese Egg Salad Sandwich (Tamago Sando). Chef John

Here's the egg salad sandwich that will have you totally reconsidering the egg salad sandwich. It's the Japanese-inspired version that has vaulted from mild-mannered convenience store snack to international sensation. One key reason is the super-savory Kewpie mayonnaise, which Chef John reproduces here. Another reason for its success is the soft, buttery, slightly sweet [Japanese milk bread](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/283940/chef-johns-milk-bread/). Bottom line, you'll never think of egg salad in the same way again.

## Ta'ameya (Egyptian Falafel)

Ta'ameya (Egyptian Falafel). Christina

"Falafel, or ta'ameya as we call it in Egypt, is an all-time favorite street food. In most parts of the Middle East, falafel is made with ground chickpeas. However, in Egypt, we make it with dried fava beans. They are best served with pita bread, tomato, onions, and tahini sauce," Gamila Salem says.

## Best Hamburger Ever

## Traditional Gyros

Kim's Cooking Now

These famous Greek sandwiches are traditionally made with a combination of meats slowly roasted on a vertical spit. This tasty skillet-made version combines seasoned ground lamb and ground beef with all the classic fixings, including [tzatziki sauce](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/232530/chef-johns-tzatziki-sauce/), the essential garlicky Greek yogurt sauce.

## Meatball Sandwich

Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

This Italian-style sandwich serves up savory meatballs seasoned with garlic, parsley, Italian seasoning, and Parmesan cheese inside a baguette for a hearty sandwich that recipe-reviewer Michelle says is "easy, excellent, and kid-approved."

## Philly Cheesesteak Sandwich with Garlic Mayo

Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

## Croque Madame

Ba

The classic "Mrs. Crunch!" This decadent French ham and melty cheese sandwich is topped with a fried egg. It reminded recipe-reviewer DarkFyre of a recent trip: "I had this when I went to Versailles and the recipe turned out almost exactly the same way."

## Chicken Pesto Paninis

JessMcKale90

Crispy focaccia bread toasted with pesto melty goodness inside — it's tough to go wrong with this Italian favorite. For even quicker preparation, Denise recommends "a rotisserie chicken from the grocery store."

## Mexican Tortas

Rosalinda Martinez

This Mexican sandwich has the works—thin-cut round steak, pinto beans, pickled jalapeños, sliced avocados and tomatoes, shredded lettuce, chopped cilantro, and crumbled queso fresco. Serve all the good stuff on bolillos rolls, the Mexican-style sandwich bread.

## Ignacio's Super Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich

Christina

Back in the early 1900s, peanut butter was considered a delicacy, and this American classic was reserved for the rich. These days, the average American will eat 1,500 PB&Js before they turn 18! This triple-decker with a toasted bread center will get you there that much faster.

## Montreal Smoked Meat Flat Wrap

Patty Cakes

In Montreal, smoked beef brisket is piled sky high onto rye bread and served with tangy mustard — the famous Montreal smoked meat sandwich. This particular version is Montreal by way of Mexico, actually. The recipe replaces rye bread with flour tortillas and adds shredded Mexican cheeses for gooey goodness. "This wrap tastes just like the one made at a Canadian restaurant, named after one of the biggest names in hockey," says Katie. "I used a Cheddar jalapeno wrap and it was very good."

## Grilled Chicken Shawarma Wraps with Raita

Bibi

"Chicken thighs grilled with shawarma seasonings are sliced and wrapped in naan, or flatbread of your choice, with a delicious raita and sandwich toppings. Serve these wrap-style sandwiches for a hearty lunch, or for dinner with some sauteed vegetables." — Bibi
