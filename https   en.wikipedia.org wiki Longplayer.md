_**Longplayer**_ is a musical composition made by British composer and musician [Jem Finer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jem_Finer) which is composed to play for 1,000 years without looping. It started to play at midnight on 1 January 2000, and if all goes as planned, it will continue without [repetition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Repetition_\(music\)) until 31 December 2999.
