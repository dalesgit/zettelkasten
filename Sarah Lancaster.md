Hello, Substack!

I am a recently retired theologian. I spent my career at a United Methodist school of theology teaching folks going into a variety of ministries. 

I would often hear students say “Why have I never heard about this before?” as they digested the work in their courses. It seems to be very hard for pastors to pass on what they know once they finish their academic preparation. So I thought in my retirement, now that there are good delivery systems like Substack, that I would try to make available some basic ideas that might not get presented to people without a formal theological education.

So the purpose of my Substack, “Thoughtful Theology: Basic Concepts,” is to introduce ideas and theological options that may not be readily accessible. In fact they may be drowned out by the loudest people who claim to speak for Christians.

Here is a taste of what you will find: “One of the main things to keep in mind in theological reflection is that God is not a “thing” in the world like other things. God is not a piece of the physical world (that is, not “empirical,” known by our physical senses). But the only language we have to think and speak about God arises from our experience of the empirical world around us, for instance, as we name the things around us and the experiences we have. Language helps us navigate the physical world. It is very useful for daily use, but it falls short when we talk about God. Our language will always be a little inappropriate for God.”

Look for me if you are interested in this kind of reflection.
