## St. Alfred's
November 2025

To the rector and senior warden of St. Alfred's Church

I am writing to you to let you know of my deep concern for St. Alfred's. The minutes of our vestry indicate that a motion was made in September that a policy be developed establishing St. Alfred's as a "gun free" facility. My understanding is that no action has yet been taken as I write this in November. 

As we are all aware, we are a nation awash with guns and gun violence. There have also been some recent decisions made in the Florida courts over the rights of our citizens to carry their guns openly. One of the contexts for my heart ache is that I have had to make difficult decisions over the course of decades of parish ministry where I applied my over-arching conviction that the church must be a **safe** environment. Difficult decisions are sometimes agonizing to make. The agony, however, doesn't lessen the vital necessity of making them. The church for me must be a **safe place**. If that is a difficult decision for the vestry of St. Alfred's church to make, I am troubled. 

I have told Fr. Peter that if I were to encounter someone at St. Alfred's with a gun I would immediately leave. That is a ditch I am willing to "die in." I may be out of touch with the culture of Florida. It may be me who is the outlier here. The diocese in which I am canonically resident -- retired as I am -- is Hawaii. That diocese passed a gun policy in 2023 which can be read on their web page at: <https://www.episcopalhawaii.org/forms--documents.html#administration>. Sample placards are available there.

My greatest concern for our parish is that the vestry has had the opportunity to establish a parish policy and has put off making a decision. That a policy for St. Alfred's to be a "gun-free" parish is not a **no brainer** decision for the vestry is for me **alarming**. 

Faithfully,

The Rev'd Dale C. Hathaway
