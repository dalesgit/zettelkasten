## St. Alfred's

I am writing to you to let you know of my deep concern for St. Alfred's. As we are all aware, we are a nation awash with guns and gun violence. There have also been some recent decisions made in the Florida courts over the rights of our citizens to carry their guns openly. One of the contexts for my heart ache is having made some difficult decisions over the course of decades of parish ministry where I applied my over-arching conviction that the church must be a **safe** environment. Difficult decisions are sometimes agonizing to make. The agony, however, doesn't lessen the vital necessity of making them. The church must be a safe place.

I have told Fr. Peter that I if I encounter someone at church with a gun I will immediately leave. It is a ditch I am willing to "die in." I may be out of touch with the culture of Florida; so it may be me who is out of touch. The diocese in which I am canonically resident -- retired as I am -- is Hawaii. That diocese passed a gun policy in 2023 which can be read on their web page at: https://www.episcopalhawaii.org/forms--documents.html#administration. An overview of the policy is:

> The Episcopal Diocese of Hawaii has declared all its property "gun-free zones" and publicly opposes gun violence. This local policy aligns with broader stances from The Episcopal Church advocating for stricter gun laws, including the regulation of firearms and prevention of gun violence. [^1]

My greatest concern for our parish is that the vestry has had the opportunity to establish a parish policy and has put off making a decision. That a policy for St. Alfred's to be a "gun-free" parish is not a **no brainer** decision for the vestry is for me **alarming**. 


[^1]: you may cf. the document at https://www.episcopalhawaii.org/uploads/2/5/4/8/25486559/gun_free_zone_letter.20230404.pdf.
