Atlanta to Denver at night. 

This tube I'm traveling in gets me hither
At 300 miles per hour. 
It's inhuman to travel at such a speed. 
But look. 
There's a port hole on this ship. 
I look out into a panorama with humans in it. 
I know so because humans lit the pin pricks
I see. The patterns of inhabitants. 
A black streak marks the path of that old river. 
The screen in front of me informs
Like an automatic Google
With names below me. 
Obscure villages. 
Patterns that map street lights. 
Thousands of narratives. 
Humans at work and leisure,
If I have enough imaginative strength
To bring them momentarily to life. 
I feel alive. Not alone. Human.
