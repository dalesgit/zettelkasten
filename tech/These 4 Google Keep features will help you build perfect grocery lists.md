## 1 Checklists

To build and manage grocery lists, all you need is Google Keep's checklist feature. Checklists work great in Keep and include all the options you need to create the ultimate shopping list, keep track of all the items you frequently buy, and [make the process of grocery shopping as painless as possible](http://www.howtogeek.com/grocery-pickup-is-the-best-tech-you-arent-using/).

When you want to create a new grocery list, the first step is creating a new checklist in Google Keep, either directly from the [Google Keep home screen widget](https://www.howtogeek.com/i-keep-forgetting-things-so-i-added-sticky-notes-to-my-androids-home-screen/) or inside the app.

Instead of deleting the list once you're done with shopping, I recommend reusing it for all your future grocery lists. It's because Google Keep keeps track of all the items you've checked, so you can easily search for an item you've previously checked and re-add it to the list.

   ![A list showing checked items in a Google Keep checklist.](https://static0.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/google-keep-shopping-lists-03.jpg?q=70&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1)

You don't even have to look for them in the checked item list. Just add a new entry and start typing out said item. If it's on the list of checked items, Keep will autocomplete the entry, and you'll be able to re-add it or create a new item. At the time of writing this, I've got around 270 entries on my checked items list ready to re-add them to my grocery list.

   ![Adding a previously checked item in a Google Keep checklist.](https://static0.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/google-keep-shopping-lists-04.jpg?q=70&fit=crop&w=750&dpr=1)

The autocomplete feature can also save you time when building grocery lists because there's a ton of groceries, dishes, and general shopping items in Google Keep's database.

   ![Adding a new entry to a Google Keep checklist.](https://static0.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/google-keep-shopping-lists-05.jpg?q=70&fit=crop&w=750&dpr=1)

## 2 Indented Sublists

Checklists in Google Keep offer several extra features that are perfect for building and refining your grocery lists.

For instance, you can create multiple indented sublists within the main checklist. These can be a list of dish ingredients, a grocery category, or a specific store where you buy certain items. To do this, create a header item you'll use as the category name, add another item underneath it, and then tap and hold the "Move" icon (the six-dot array) to the right to indent it.

   ![A sublist created inside a Google Keep checklists.](https://static0.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/google-keep-shopping-lists-06.jpg?q=70&fit=crop&w=750&dpr=1)

I use this feature to create ingredient lists for dishes I often cook, as well as separate grocery sublists for shopping at different stores.

### How to Edit and Manage Indented Sublists

If you want to move a sublist up or down the main checklist, just tap and hold the header item and move it up or down, which will in turn move the entire sublist.

Another convenient feature keeps items that are part of a sublist tied to that sublist when they move to the bottom once you check them. So when you uncheck them, they will return as part of the same sublist.

   ![A list of checked items in a Google Keep checklist.](https://static0.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/google-keep-shopping-lists-09.jpg?q=70&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1)

You can also check the header item once you buy all the items in the sublist, and the entire sublist will be archived to the checked items list. Then just uncheck the header item and you'll get the whole sublist back!

To remove a single item or sub-item from the list, tap it and then tap the "X" button.

   ![Deleting a checked item from a Google Keep checklist.](https://static0.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/google-keep-shopping-lists-15.jpg?q=70&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1)

## 3 Adding Photos and Recordings to Grocery Lists

You can add multiple photos, images, or recordings to your grocery list. This can be super handy when you aren't sure how a product looks, in which aisle it's located, or for other reasons.

You can add photos and images by tapping the "Plus" sign and then tapping the "Take photo" or "Add image" options. You can also create drawings or add audio recordings.

Google Keep also allows annotating photos and images. To annotate an image, tap it and then tap the "Edit" button.

   ![Annotating an image in Google Keep.](https://static0.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/google-keep-shopping-lists-12.jpg?q=70&fit=crop&w=750&dpr=1)

## 4 Adding Collaborators to Your Grocery Lists

If you and your partner (or roommate, children, or anyone else) share grocery shopping duties, just add them as a collaborator so you can build and manage grocery lists together, as well as coordinate when shopping.

To add a collaborator (or multiple collaborators), tap the three-dot menu.

   ![Different menu buttons in Google Keep.](https://static0.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/google-keep-shopping-lists-13.jpg?q=70&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1)

Next, just tap the "Collaborator" button.

   ![Three-dot menu button options in Google Keep.](https://static0.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/google-keep-shopping-lists-14.jpg?q=70&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1)

### There's One Missing Feature That Would be Very Handy for Grocery Lists

Google Keep is a [surprisingly powerful](http://www.howtogeek.com/i-use-google-keep-daily-and-these-5-features-deserve-more-attention/) and [feature-rich](http://www.howtogeek.com/google-keeps-best-updates/) tool for building and managing grocery lists. Its checklist feature includes a variety of ways of building shopping lists that contain different categories and sublists.

    ![A phone above a notebook with the Google Keep logo and some sticky notes beside it.](https://static0.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/a-phone-above-a-notebook-with-the-google-keep-logo-and-some-sticky-notes-beside-it.jpg?q=49&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=2)Credit: Lucas Gouveia / How-To Geek | Prathankarnpap / [Shuttterstock](https://www.shutterstock.com/image-photo/horizontal-photo-smart-phone-blank-screen-1947580246)

Powerful and easy to use as it is, Google Keep doesn't support multi-level checklists. While you can create indented sublists, you cannot create nested sub-categories inside sublists.

This feature might sound like overkill, but I'd love to be able to add grocery categories to my store sublists or group multiple dishes into a single weekly shopping list. At the moment, though, that feature's missing, and it doesn't look like it will be added soon.

Now, I could create multiple checklists and nest sublists inside them, but I find that too messy. I prefer having all my grocery shopping lists and items in one master checklist that includes every item I've ever created, so I can easily browse, search, and uncheck items when needed.
