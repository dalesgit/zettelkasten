# Queen Emma of Hawaii

Queen of Hawaii from 1856 to 1863

* * *

**Emma Kalanikaumakaʻamano Kaleleonālani Naʻea Rooke**(January 2, 1836 -- April 25, 1885) was queen of [Hawaii](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaiian_Kingdom) as the wife of King [Kamehameha IV](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamehameha_IV) from 1856 to his death in 1863. She was later known for being a [humanitarian](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanitarianism), establishing a hospital, and was an unsuccessful candidate for the throne. For her missionary activities she is [venerated](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feast_of_the_Holy_Sovereigns)as a holy woman in the American [Episcopal Church](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Episcopal_Church_\(United_States\)).
