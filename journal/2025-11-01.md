## End of life at St. Alfred's #end-of-life
- off the top $30k if have to go to probate
- "revocable trust"
- retitling assets in the name of a revocable trust.
- with no other plans, if a spouse dies, ½ will go to spouse, the other ½ will go to biological children (not step children)
- he seems to advocate strongly for the "revocable" thing -- which becomes *irrevocable* on death.
- reevaluate will every year
- typically the demands/needs of "modern families" don't align with what Florida legislators intended in the statutes.
- e.g. If both have accident, surviving spouse gets everything. That spouse dies everything goes to that spouse's children. [first spouse's children get nothing]
