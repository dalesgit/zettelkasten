**Liliʻuokalani** 
(Hawaiian pronunciation:[[liˌliʔuokəˈlɐni]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/Hawaiian); Lydia Liliʻu Loloku Walania Kamakaʻeha; September 2, 1838 -- November 11, 1917) was the only [queen regnant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_regnant) and the last sovereign monarch of the [Hawaiian Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaiian_Kingdom), ruling from January 29, 1891, until the [overthrow of the Hawaiian Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overthrow_of_the_Hawaiian_Kingdom) on January 17, 1893, in a coup that was led by the [Committee of Safety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_of_Safety_\(Hawaii\)), composed of seven foreign residents (five Americans, one Scotsman, and one German) and six Hawaiian Kingdom subjects of American descent in [Honolulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honolulu).[^cite_ref-FOOTNOTEBlount1895588_1-0][[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lili%CA%BBuokalani#cite_note-FOOTNOTEBlount1895588-1)[^cite_ref-FOOTNOTEKam201931--54_2-0][[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lili%CA%BBuokalani#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKam201931%E2%80%9354-2) The composer of "[Aloha ʻOe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aloha_%CA%BBOe)" and numerous other works, she wrote her autobiography _[Hawaiʻi's Story by Hawaiʻi's Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaii%27s_Story_by_Hawaii%27s_Queen)_(1898) during her imprisonment following the overthrow.
