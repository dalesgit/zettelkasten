**Total Lunar Eclipse in Tampa, FL -- March 3, 2026**

The total lunar eclipse in Tampa, Florida, began at **3:44a.m. ET** on Tuesday, March 3, 2026, when the Earth's penumbral shadow first touched the Moon.
- **Penumbral Eclipse Begins**: 3:44 a.m. ET -- Subtle dimming of the Moon starts.
- **Partial Eclipse Begins**: 4:50 a.m. ET -- A dark shadow begins to appear on the Moon.
- **Total Eclipse Begins (Blood Moon)**: 6:04 a.m. ET -- The Moon is fully within Earth's umbra and turns red.
- **Maximum Eclipse**: 6:33 a.m. ET -- The Moon is at the center of Earth's shadow, appearing deepest red.
- **Total Eclipse Ends**: 7:02 a.m. ET -- The Moon starts exiting the umbra.
- **Moonset**: Around 6:54 a.m. ET -- The Moon sets below the horizon during totality, so viewing is limited to the early phase.

The eclipse ends before sunrise, which occurs at approximately 6:43 a.m. ET. For best viewing, look low on the **western horizon** with a clear view. No special equipment is needed--**the eclipse is safe to view with the naked eye**.
