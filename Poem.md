Poem

Whence my wonder at words?
In the beginning? Which?
Hebrew or Greek? Philo?
Kierkegaard? Yeats?

For years I memorized poetry. 
I'm long past able to perform. 
After years of trying to connect
Muscle memory in my fingers with
Ear muscles poorly trained
I last week re-discovered the
Purcell piece that saw me through
Long years of fallow
For hearing the word. 

Awe. Ah, yes. 

For the word was at the birth of the beginning  
It made the heavens and the earth and set them spinning  
And for several million years  
It's endured all our forums and fine ideas  
It's been rough  
It's been rough but it appears to be winning!

Bernstein frolicked with the word. 
Winning no? We're not. But the words?
The word!

Yes.
