# Diabetes Dietician course
## Overview
- Benefits: e.g. no daily decisions. Better numbers.
>
## Accept
- [~] takes time, planning
- a life skill (eating 3x's every day)
- similar to learning to drive (independence)
- [ ] like brushing teeth or doing laundry
- learn what makes my stomach upset. **Food is information**.
- self-care task 

## Goals for every meal 
- satisfy hunger
- provide energy (adequate carbohydrates)
- Build/maintain mnuscle (adequate) protein
- Prevent blood sugar spikes (adequate fiber. Balance of protein & carbs)
- Taste, flavor (include healthy fat, herbs or spices)
- **not** for emotional therapy, coping skill

## Recommendations
- TDEE calculate calorie needs
- general re. carbs: less 200, 150, … 
- protein 0.8-1.1 (per kgm)-- 25%-30% of total calories 
- https://tdeecalculator.net/
- **calories** (n.b. that calorie needs are very much individualized)
- 2,300 mg **salt** per day

## divided plate balance

- n.b. starchy vegetables
- some evidence for eating starchy veggies **last**, so do fiber and protein first
- healthy carbs. Things gramma would recognize
- **protein** allows to build muscle mass
- **fiber** whole grains, starchy vegetables, beans & lentils, fruit, nuts & seeds, non-starchy vegetables (**only in plant based foods**)
- **flavor: fat** what is too much? -- 1-3 'servings' / meal to start … 
- **mounjaro** if very fatty foods it may increase nausea
- cp. McKormic non-salt vs. Mrs. Dash. Onion powder is good. Onion salt not so good. cf. hidden sodium.

## meals

- overdoing carbs (spaghetti e.g.) 1-2 hours later feeling lethargic and tired
