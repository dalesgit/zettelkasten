Recently, we reported on a pretty mind-blowing study published in the [Canadian Journal of Cardiology](https://www.onlinecjc.ca/article/S0828-282X\(22\)00213-6/fulltext) that suggested improved cardiac function in participants who committed to Nordic [walking](https://www.huffpost.com/topic/walking), which involves walking sticks or trekking poles.

Compared to normal walking, the addition of poles or sticks forces the walker to engage the upper body muscles, as well as the lower body, for more well-rounded movement. (You can learn more about Nordic walking in our [previous coverage](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/nordic-walking-poles-benefits-ano_l_67a399dde4b009cdb97ae214).)

Boosted heart health aside, we learned from tons of people who purchased the [TrailBuddy hiking sticks](https://r.bttn.io/?btn_ref=org-677cb81987ed72f9&btn_url=https://www.amazon.com/TrailBuddy-Trekking-Poles-Lightweight-Collapsible/dp/B07CC49R4C?tag=tessaflores-20%26ascsubtag%3D68d5875ae4b0b2f96b454a2f%252C-1%252C-1%252Cd%252C0%252C0%252Chp-fil-am%253D0%252C0%253A0%252C0%252C0%252C0) — which are the highest-rated pair of trekking poles on [Amazon](https://www.huffpost.com/life/topic/amazon) — that they offer additional benefits to walking routines, especially for those who may find this vital form of [exercise](https://www.huffpost.com/life/topic/fitness) challenging to begin with.

## According to an overwhelming majority, these collapsible and well-constructed trekking poles make walking easier, safer, and, even improve posture, thus making walking a more comfortable experience.

For instance, several reviewers who claimed to have prior injuries or other health conditions that make walking difficult consider [the TrailBuddy sticks](https://r.bttn.io/?btn_ref=org-677cb81987ed72f9&btn_url=https://www.amazon.com/TrailBuddy-Trekking-Poles-Lightweight-Collapsible/dp/B0DP1KQNKY?tag=tessaflores-20%26ascsubtag%3D68d5875ae4b0b2f96b454a2f%252C-1%252C-1%252Cd%252C0%252C0%252Chp-fil-am%253D0%252C0%253A0%252C0%252C0%252C0) an affordable way to achieve their walking and health goals confidently and independently.

Made from aircraft-grade aluminum and weighing less than 10 ounces each, the TrailBuddy poles are height-adjustable up to 54 inches to accommodate most statures. When not fully extended, the poles collapse to 24.5 inches or disassemble to just 21 inches, making them easy to store or take along on trips where there might be a lot of walking required. The lever locks on each pole also help ensure they’re secure enough to bear weight and only require the use of one hand to adjust between heights.

This TrailBuddy set comes with three sets of different tip attachments so no matter if you’re using these poles to walk on rocky terrain, muddy trails, or just your neighborhood sidewalk, these walking sticks have got you covered. One reviewer even claimed they use these to walk securely on icy and snowy surfaces during a Wisconsin winter.

You can also expect another simple yet thoughtful design feature with the TrailBuddy poles: padded wrist straps on each of the moisture-wicking cork handles to add a bit more support.
