From Fr. Peter:

Marc, husband of Rebecca LaReau, died last Saturday after having been in ill health for some time. He and Rebecca, longtime members of the 5:00 p.m. Saturday service, both served as Lectors.

  

Marc had been active in the arts as a performer and educator for over 45 years. In 1991 he founded and was the Director of Acting Opera. In 2015 he wrote and published a book on acting. But he and Rebecca also used their talents for the benefit of St. Alfred's during the fundraising phase of our pipe organ project. Together, Marc as piano accompanist and Rebecca as soprano soloist presented two Sunday afternoon musical programs. On August 8, 2021, they offered Biblical Songs, Opus 99, a collection of 10 psalms set to music by Antonin Dvorak. A little over a year later, on October 9, 2022, they presented Thanks be to God: A Program of Song.

  

Arrangements have not yet been made for Marc's service. We will notify the parish when we have that information. Condolences may be addressed to Rebecca at:
