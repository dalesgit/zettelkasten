Are you someone who tends to question everything? [Analyze](https://parade.com/living/analysis-paralysis) information deeply? Look for connections? If this sounds familiar—or perhaps you know someone that we're describing—then it's possible that they may be a [high-level thinker](https://parade.com/living/high-level-thinking-phrases-according-to-psychologists).  
  
"Usually when people are talking about high-level [thinkers](https://parade.com/1288259/marynliles/lateral-thinking-puzzles/), this refers to someone who possesses and shows critical thinking skills, keen judgment and an ability to problem solve (effectively) that is above and beyond the average person's capacity or openness," says **[Dr. Ayanna Abrams](https://www.ascensionbehavioralhealth.com/),** a licensed clinical psychologist in Atlanta, GA. "They appear to be more creative, play around with more nuance, read into overlooked details in ways that can expand and deepen discussions about various topics."  
  
This is just the tip of the iceberg when it comes to high-level thinkers and the [qualities](https://parade.com/astrology/most-intelligent-intuitive-zodiac-signs) that make them unique and special. People with this level of analysis spot patterns and minute details that the average person might miss. So *Parade* spoke with clinical psychologists about people whose brain works like this, along with **things high-level thinkers [notice that others don't](https://parade.com/living/toxic-phrases-emotionally-intelligent-people-notice-according-to-psychologists)**.

## What Is a High-Level Thinker?

A high-level thinker is a person who tends to be naturally [reflective](https://parade.com/living/shadow-work-prompts) and curious about life. As Dr. Abrams said, they think critically, have really good judgment and can elevate conversations, among other things.  
  
"Instead of simply accepting information at face value, they are more likely to extrapolate abstractly, consider how things fit into larger patterns or systems and question the deeper 'why' behind what we see on the surface," says [**Dr. Patricia Dixon**](https://patriciasdixon.com/), licensed clinical psychologist in Palmetto, FL. "You might say that they tend to look for meaning behind the obvious, connect the dots between ideas to see the bigger picture or future possibilities, think 'outside the box' and weigh multiple perspectives."  
  
In the same vein, high-level thinkers operate from a [growth-based mindset](https://parade.com/1172081/marynliles/growth-mindset/). They're constantly seeking deeper understanding, better questions and more empowering perspectives, according to [**Dr. Byron McClure**](https://www.byronmcclure.com/), certified school psychologist and director of innovation at [7 Mindsets](https://7mindsets.com/).  
  
When it comes to being a high-level thinker, although some people may seem to have a more natural knack for this way of seeing the world, it's actually a quality that can be honed and developed with practice.  
  
"By facing complexity, questioning our beliefs and staying curious, you can open the door to deeper understanding and broader perspective," says [**Dr. Konstantin Lukin**](https://www.lukincenter.com/people/konstantin-lukin-ph-d/), licensed clinical psychologist and the founder of [Lukin Center for Psychotherapy](https://www.lukincenter.com/). "Anyone can strengthen this way of thinking by exploring new ideas and experiences."

## 9 Things High-Level Thinkers Always Notice, According to Psychologists

### 1\. Deeper meaning in everyday situations

According to Dr. Dixon, high-level thinkers notice when something is “more than it seems” and are curious about the significance of what they’re observing.  
  
"This could mean linking behavior to motive, tying actions to values or context or seeking out any insight into why a person or group is acting the way they do," she tells *Parade.*  
  
For example, a high-level thinker might come across a challenge or a [tough time](https://parade.com/1012592/kelseypelzer/quotes-for-tough-times/), and instead of viewing it as a setback, they'll infer a deeper meaning.  
  
"They instinctively ask, 'What is this teaching me?' viewing adversity as a teacher rather than a threat," Dr. McClure says. "At their core, they operate from a belief in possibility and the idea that every experience can be used to move forward."

### 2\. Multiple ways to solve a problem

According to **[Dr. Vanessa Kennedy](https://driftwoodrecovery.com/our-team/vanessa-s-kennedy-phd)** —chief psychologist at [Driftwood Recovery](https://driftwoodrecovery.com/) —high-level thinkers often notice that there may be multiple ways to [solve](https://parade.com/947956/parade/riddles/) something.  
  
"They question the status quo and understand that being open to different ways of thinking enhances the problem-solving process," she explains. "They are able to listen to others to generate new ideas and creative solutions, and they are willing to change their approach when presented with a more effective idea."

### 3\. How to create deeper connections with people

For high-level thinkers, [small talk](https://parade.com/living/most-common-small-talk-mistake-according-to-jefferson-fisher) is the bane of their existence. They're not interested in superficial bonds with people and will strive for more meaningful conversations and connections.  
  
"They listen to what’s not being said, seek alignment and invest in mutual growth," Dr. McClure says. "They understand that relationships aren’t optional in life or leadership—they’re essential and recognize the potential that exists within every person they encounter."  
  
If you find yourself wanting to get to know someone on a deeper level, aside from pleasantries, when you meet someone, then you just might be a high-level thinker.

### 4\. The importance of learning as an ongoing process

You know the saying, "You [learn](https://parade.com/living/lessons-for-kids-to-learn-according-to-child-psychologists) something new every day"? Well, high-level thinkers are the living definition of this phrase.  
  
"High-level thinkers notice that learning never stops and they seek out information from a variety of sources that may seem obscure to others," Dr. Kennedy says. "They understand that various groups of people, time periods and fields of study have yielded knowledge and important lessons relevant to human progress."  
  
Oftentimes, high-level thinkers show a great deal of interest in school because they are always looking to learn and expand their knowledge of the world around them.

### 5\. The bigger picture

It's easy to fixate on the small inconveniences or trivial problems of life; however, high-level thinkers tend to notice how smaller details or actions are a part of—or contribute to—larger patterns, trends and outcomes.  
  
"They consider the ripple effects and consequences, instead of just the immediate data in front of them," Dr. Dixon says.

### 6\. The importance of tolerating uncertainty

According to Dr. Kennedy, high-level thinkers typically notice that they are comfortable with the ambiguity and uncertainty of risk.  
  
"They are able to tolerate multiple problems at once and allow unanswered questions to hang in the balance without rushing to solve them," she explains. "This allows them to be thoughtful about considering various solutions and prioritizing ideas that make the greatest impact."

### 7\. How to easily 'read a room'

High-level thinkers are more likely to notice [subtler non-verbal](https://parade.com/living/classy-nonverbal-cues-according-to-etiquette-expert) or emotional cues that can be used to “read” a situation.  
  
"This may include small facial movements, changes in tone or pacing and shifts in breathing," Dr. Dixon says. "These cues can offer key insights into how people are feeling and what’s really going on behind the scenes."

### 8\. Their introspective thoughts and intuition

Do you find yourself constantly questioning, or just noticing, your inner thoughts or beliefs? That could be because you're a high-level thinker. Dr. McClure says they tend to observe their self-talk, question their beliefs and upgrade their thoughts regularly.  
  
"High-level thinkers know that unchecked thinking can [quietly sabotage progress](https://parade.com/living/subtle-self-sabotaging-habits-according-to-psychologist), so they stay intentional with their mindset," he explains. "They practice accountability at a deep level, owning their internal world just as much as their external actions."

### 9\. The importance of the present moment

Instead of waiting to do something tomorrow or later, high-level thinkers act in the present.  
  
"They are aware that every moment is a decision point," Dr. McClaure says. "They don’t wait for ideal conditions or perfect timing, but they act when the opportunity is in front of them, recognizing that meaningful change begins with what they choose to do right now."
