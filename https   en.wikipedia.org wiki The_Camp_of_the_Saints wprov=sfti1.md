On its publication, the book received praise from some prominent French literary figures, and through time has also been praised by some critics and politicians in Europe and the United States, but has also been criticized by both French and English-language commentators for conveying [racist](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racist), [xenophobic](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xenophobic), [nativist](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nativism_\(politics\)), and [anti-immigration](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-immigration) themes. The novel is popular within [far-right](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far-right) and [white nationalist](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_nationalist) circles.
